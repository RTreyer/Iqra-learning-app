# Math Unit 2 (numbers 0 to 10): design

Date: 2026-11-05. Status: approved by the owner. Step 2 of the math rebuild (see `2026-11-05-math-foundation-design.md`).

Source: الرياضيات، الصف الأول، الجزء الأول (Sana'a 2026), Unit 2 الأعداد (٠ - ١٠), printed pp. 33 to 64. Notes: `docs/content/textbooks/math-part1/unit-02.md`.

## Foundation additions

1. **Ordered rows in `picture_choice`.** `PictureChoiceRound.ordered?: boolean`. When true the choices are not shuffled, they are shown as one row in reading order (first on the right), small, up to 10 choices; used for ordinal numbers ("which one is 4th?"). Validator: 2 to `MAX_CHOICES` choices normally, 2 to 10 when `ordered`.
2. **Zero.** A `picture_choice` round may have at most one empty choice; it shows an empty card labelled فَارِغٌ. `compare` sides stay non empty.
3. **Digit tracing.** Trace guides for ٠ to ٩ and ١٠ are generated by `scripts/build-trace-guides.mjs` (the Arabic range already covers Arabic-Indic digits).

## Unit `math-u2`

File `content/math/bookUnit2.ts`, built with `mathKit.ts`. Unit number 2, title الْوَحْدَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ, theme الأَعْدَادُ مِنْ ٠ إِلَى ١٠. Every lesson has a `section` from `bookSection(2, n, title)`.

| Book lesson | App lessons (type) |
|---|---|
| 2-1 الأعداد ١، ٢، ٣ | count and pick (`counting`); which group has this many (`picture_choice`); write ١ ٢ ٣ (`trace`) |
| 2-2 الأعداد ٤، ٥، ٠ | same three, zero as the empty group and count 0 |
| 2-3 الأعداد ٦، ٧، ٨ | same three |
| 2-4 العددان ٩، ١٠ | same three |
| 2-5 تدريبات | two mixed counting lessons (0 to 10) |
| 2-6 مقارنة الأعداد | groups with words (`compare`); numbers with symbols (`compare`); bigger or smaller of two numbers (`compare`, words mode with numbers: أَكْبَرُ / أَصْغَرُ) |
| 2-7 ترتيب الأعداد | missing numbers in rows (`number_fill` sequence) |
| 2-8 الأعداد الترتيبية | the five girls boarding the bus (أَرْوَى، بِلْقِيسُ، فَاطِمَةُ، سَارَةُ، زَيْنَبُ) and the ten animals: tap the nth (`picture_choice` ordered) |
| 2-9 حل المسألة | the maze: ٠ to ١٠ with gaps (`number_fill` sequence) |
| 2-10 تدريبات عامة | counting in 2s forwards and backwards (`number_fill` sequence); < > = (`compare` symbols) |
| اختبار الوحدة | one mixed lesson |

Left out: the bar chart (enrichment), counting letters in words, dot to dot, colouring.

Content rules: Eastern Arabic digits; prompts in fully vowelled Arabic using the book's terms (أَعُدُّ، أُحَوِّطُ، أَصِلُ، أَكْبَرُ مِنْ، أَصْغَرُ مِنْ، يُسَاوِي، الأَوَّلُ … الْعَاشِرُ); 3 to 6 rounds per lesson; pictures are emoji, Yemeni where possible (✈️ Yemenia aircraft, 🌾 sorghum, 🇾🇪 flags, 🐦 hoopoe stamp). The `<` sign follows the book: the open side faces the bigger number, `first` is on the right (`CompareSymbol`).

## Replacing the old units

- Old `m1` (counting, `content/math/unit1.ts`) is removed; its progress is dropped (decided in the foundation spec).
- Old `m2` and `m3` stay until steps 3 to 5. They are renumbered to the book units they cover: `m2` becomes number 3 titled الْوَحْدَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ (it covers book Units 3 and 4), `m3` number 5 titled الْوَحْدَةُ الْخَامِسَةُ. Their lessons are unchanged.
- Catalog order: `math-u2`, `m2`, `m3`.
