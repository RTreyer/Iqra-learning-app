# Unit 9: الْوَحْدَةُ التَّاسِعَةُ (new letters ك، خ، ذ)

Printed pp. 73 to 105 (PDF pp. 74 to 106). Three lessons, then تدريبات. First unit that is new in Part 2.

Unit cover (p. 73): an open book with: كَ، كَا، كِ، كِي، كُ، كُو، خَ، خَا، كْ (right page) and خِ، خِي، خُ، خُو، خْ، ذَ، ذِ، ذُ، ذْ (left page).

## Lesson structure (changes from Units 7 and 8 of Part 2)

Each lesson has 10 pages:
1. Title page (letter badge, picture).
2. Story text (fully printed, key letters in red).
3. Colouring page (the letter drawn into an object).
4. Reading page fatha + long alif.
5. Writing page (four letter forms, four words).
6. Reading page damma + long و.
7. **New: dictation and picture naming page.** Class part: ١- أَنَا أَكْتُبُ مَا يُمْلَى (write two words the teacher dictates), ٢- أَنَا أَكْتُبُ مَرَّةً أُخْرَى (write them again). Homework part: أَنَا أَكْتُبُ اسْمَ الصُّورَةِ (write the name of the picture): one solved example and two photos to name.
8. Reading page kasra + long ي.
9. Build the word (أَنَا أُرَكِّبُ), as in Units 7 and 8.
10. Reading page sukun, ending with the singing bird.

App mapping: dictation → "hear a word, then build it from letter tiles" (spelling); picture naming → "see a picture, build or pick its written name".

## 9-1 الْكِتَابُ صَدِيقِي (letter ك), pp. 74 to 83

### p. 74, title page
Letter badge: ك. Picture: a boy in a white jumper with blue stripes kneels on a pavement next to a red wall and picks up a torn, dirty book lying on the ground; a road and a house with arched windows behind.

### p. 75, story text (fully printed)
- بَيْنَمَا حَكِيمٌ يَسِيرُ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ، سَمِعَ صَوْتًا يُنَادِي: يَا حَكِيمُ، يَا حَكِيمُ، اقْتَرَبَ حَكِيمٌ مِنْ مَكَانِ الصَّوْتِ، فَوَجَدَ كِتَابًا بِجَانِبِ الطَّرِيقِ.
- قَالَ الْكِتَابُ: أَشْكُو إِلَيْكَ حَالِي! صَدِيقُكَ كَمَالٌ رَمَانِي.
- قَالَ حَكِيمٌ: لَا تَحْزَنْ، سَوْفَ أَضَعُكَ فِي مَكْتَبَةِ الْبَيْتِ، وَسَتَكُونُ صَدِيقِي، فَأَنَا أُحِبُّ قِرَاءَةَ الْكُتُبِ.

Red letters: حَكِيمٌ، مَكَانِ، كِتَابًا، أَشْكُو، كَمَالٌ، مَكْتَبَةِ، الْكُتُبِ.
Value: respecting books, love of reading. A talking book (personification).

### p. 76, colouring
The letter ك drawn as an armchair (the upright back is the stem of ك, the small hamza-like mark on the seat). Coloured model and outline.

### p. 77, reading كَـ - كَا
1. كَـ، ـكَـ، ـكَ، كَ، كَا، ـكَا
2. كَـ/مَا/لُ → كَمَالُ ; مَـ/كَا/نُ → مَكَانُ
3. Sight words: يُنَادِي، صَدِيقِي
4. Listening picture: the peacock on a mound with birds on the branches of a big tree (same as Part 1 Unit 7, p. 162). Fable of the birds choosing a king. Text not printed.

### p. 78, writing ك
Forms: كـ، ـكـ، ـك، ك. Words: كتاب، مكتبة، شبك، يبارك. Class and homework.

### p. 79, reading كُـ - كُو
1. كُـ، ـكُـ، ـكُ، كُ، كُو، ـكُو
2. الْـ/كُـ/تُـ/بُ → الْكُتُبُ ; أَشْـ/كُو → أَشْكُو
3. Sight word: سَمِعَ
4. **Speaking picture**: a school library; a boy in a blue uniform browses a book at a table with piles of books; a girl in a white headscarf reads a magazine; other pupils take books from shelves.

### p. 80, dictation and picture naming
Class: dictation boxes (2 words) and write again (2 words); the dictated words are not printed (teacher chooses, probably from the lesson).
Homework picture names: كَلْب (photo of a grey dog, solved example), كُرَة (football), مَكْتَبَة (bookshelf with books).

### p. 81, reading كِـ - كِي
1. كِـ، ـكِـ، ـكِ، كِ، كِيـ، ـكِيـ، كِي، ـكِي
2. كِـ/تَا/بُ → كِتَابُ ; حَـ/كِيـ/مُ → حَكِيمُ
3. Sight word: أَضَعُكَ
4. Listening picture: a fox runs away down a hill with a bell tied around its neck; two boys at a house door point at it (same as Part 1 Unit 2, p. 48). Fable of the fox with the bell. Text not printed.

### p. 82, build the word
Class: الْ كُـ تُـ بُ → الكتب and كُـ تُـ بُ الْ (scrambled); مَـ كَا نُ → مكان and كَا نُ مَـ. Homework: حَـ كِيـ مُ → حكيم and كِيـ حَـ مُ; كَـ مَا لُ → كمال and مَا كَـ لُ.

### p. 83, reading كْ (sukun)
1. كْـ، ـكْـ، ـكْ، كْ
2. مَكْـ/تَـ/بَـ/ةُ → مَكْتَبَةُ
3. Sight word: رَمَانِي
4. Singing bird.

## 9-2 اللهُ لَطِيفٌ (letter خ), pp. 84 to 93

### p. 84, title page
Letter badge: خ. Picture: a ship tossed by huge waves in a storm at sea, heavy rain and dark clouds.

### p. 85, story text (fully printed)
- كَانَتْ بَاخِرَةٌ تَسِيرُ فِي الْبَحْرِ، وَفَجْأَةً ظَهَرَتْ عَاصِفَةٌ مُخِيفَةٌ. خَافَ رُكَّابُ الْبَاخِرَةِ، وَرَاحُوا يَصْرُخُونَ.
- خَرَجَ قُبْطَانُ الْبَاخِرَةِ، وَقَالَ: لَا تَخَافُوا، وَأَرْجُو أَنْ يَدْخُلَ الْجَمِيعُ، وَلَا يَخْرُجْ أَحَدٌ.
- دَعَا رُكَّابُ الْبَاخِرَةِ: يَا اللهُ .. يَا لَطِيفُ ..!، فَتَوَقَّفَتْ عَاصِفَةُ الْبَحْرِ، قَالَ الْجَمِيعُ: الْحَمْدُ لِلّهِ.

Red letters: بَاخِرَةٌ، مُخِيفَةٌ، خَافَ، يَصْرُخُونَ، خَرَجَ، يَدْخُلَ، يَخْرُجْ.
Value: trust in God, following instructions in danger.

### p. 86, colouring
The letter خ drawn on a woolly sheep (خَرُوف), the dot of خ on its head. Coloured model and outline.

### p. 87, reading خَـ - خَا
1. خَـ، ـخَـ، ـخَ، خَ، خَا، ـخَا
2. خَـ/رَ/جَ → خَرَجَ ; خَا/فَ → خَافَ
3. Sight word: الْجَمِيعُ
4. Listening picture: a fox in a jacket and scarf knocks at the door of a house with a red crescent sign (a clinic); a rooster in a doctor's role looks out of the window, worried. Fable of the fox pretending to be ill to get to the rooster. Text not printed.

### p. 88, writing خ
Forms: خـ، ـخـ، ـخ، خ. Words: خروف، بخور، ينفخ، صاروخ. Class and homework.

### p. 89, reading خُـ - خُو
1. خُـ، ـخُـ، ـخُ، خُ، خُو، ـخُو
2. يَدْ/خُـ/لُ → يَدْخُلُ ; يَصْـ/رُ/خُو/نَ → يَصْرُخُونَ
3. Sight words: عَاصِفَةٌ، قُبْطَانُ
4. **Speaking picture**: a fisherman in a futa casts a net from a yellow boat at sea; steamships and a small boat in the distance, seagulls.

### p. 90, dictation and picture naming
Class: dictation and write again (words not printed).
Homework picture names: خُبْز (round flat bread, solved example), خَاتَم (ring with a purple stone), خَرُوف (brown sheep).

### p. 91, reading خِـ - خِي
1. خِـ، ـخِـ، ـخِ، خِ، خِيـ، ـخِيـ، خِي، ـخِي
2. بَا/خِـ/رَ/ةُ → بَاخِرَةُ ; مُـ/خِيـ/فَـ/ةُ → مُخِيفَةُ
3. Sight word: دَعَا
4. Listening picture: a man in a red-checked head cloth pours grain into a mill from a brick; a girl in a pink headscarf reaches toward the machine (same as Part 1 Unit 6, p. 126). Safety story. Text not printed.

### p. 92, build the word
Class: بَا خِـ رَ ةُ → باخرة and خِـ رَ ةُ بَا; مُـ خِيـ فَـ ةُ → مخيفة and خِيـ فَـ مُـ ةُ. Homework: يَخْ رُ جُ → يخرج and جُ يَخْ رُ; يَدْ خُـ لُ → يدخل and يَدْ لُ خُـ.

### p. 93, reading خْ (sukun)
1. خْـ، ـخْـ، ـخْ، خْ
2. يَخْ/رُ/جُ → يَخْرُجُ
3. Sight word: ظَهَرَتْ
4. Singing bird.

## 9-3 الأَكْلُ الْمَكْشُوفُ (letter ذ), pp. 94 to 103

### p. 94, title page
Letter badge: ذ. Picture: outside a school, a street vendor in a red-checked head cloth sells fried food from an open green cart; flies buzz around the food; a girl in a white headscarf with a red backpack pays, a boy buys, another boy walks away eating from a paper plate.

### p. 95, story text (fully printed)
- أَخَذَ الْمُعَلِّمُ ذِيَابًا إِلَى الطَّبِيبَةِ أَذْكَارَ.
- فَحَصَتِ الطَّبِيبَةُ ذِيَابًا، وَقَالَتْ: مَاذَا أَكَلْتَ؟
- قَالَ ذِيَابٌ: بَطَاطَا مِنْ بَائِعٍ أَمَامَ الْمَدْرَسَةِ.
- أَذَابَتِ الطَّبِيبَةُ الدَّوَاءَ، وَقَالَتْ: الْأَكْلُ الْمَكْشُوفُ يَجْمَعُ ذُبَابًا، وَيُؤْذِي صِحَّتَنَا.
- قَالَ ذِيَابٌ: لَنْ أَذُوقَ الْأَكْلَ الْمَكْشُوفَ مَرَّةً أُخْرَى.

Red letters: أَخَذَ، ذِيَابًا، أَذْكَارَ، أَذَابَتِ، ذُبَابًا، يُؤْذِي، أَذُوقَ.
Value: food hygiene (do not eat uncovered street food).

### p. 96, colouring
The letter ذ drawn on a corn cob (ذُرَة). Coloured model and outline.

### p. 97, reading ذَ - ذَا
1. ذَ، ـذَ، ذَا، ـذَا
2. أَ/خَـ/ذَ → أَخَذَ ; أَ/ذَا/بَتْ → أَذَابَتْ
3. Sight word: يَجْمَعُ
4. Listening picture: the hyena playing a flute while two goats dance (same as Part 1 Unit 8, p. 184). Fable. Text not printed.

### p. 98, writing ذ
Non-connecting: ذ، ـذ (alternating). Words: ذباب، يذوب، يأخذ، رذاذ. Class and homework.

### p. 99, reading ذُ - ذُو
1. ذُ، ـذُ، ذُو، ـذُو
2. ذُ/بَا/بُ → ذُبَابُ ; أَ/ذُو/قُ → أَذُوقُ
3. Sight word: الدَّوَاءُ
4. **Speaking picture**: a football pitch; a boy in an orange kit helps up a boy in a white kit who has fallen; a coach in a yellow shirt hurries over; other players running.

### p. 100, dictation and picture naming
Class: dictation and write again (words not printed).
Homework picture names: ذُرَة (corn cob, solved example), ذُبَاب (a fly), ذَيْل (an animal's tail).

### p. 101, reading ذِ - ذِي
1. ذِ، ـذِ، ذِيـ، ـذِيـ، ـذِي، ذِي
2. ذِ/يَا/بُ → ذِيَابُ ; يُؤْ/ذِي → يُؤْذِي
3. Sight word: بَائِعٌ
4. Listening picture: a boy leans out of a moving train and throws his second shoe onto the platform where the first already lies (same as Part 1 Unit 7, p. 150). Text not printed.

### p. 102, build the word
Class: أَ خَـ ذَ → أخذ and ذَ أَ خَـ; ذُ بَا بُ → ذباب and ذُ بُ بَا. Homework: أَ ذُو قُ → أذوق and ذُو قُ أَ; أَذْ كَا رُ → أذكار and أَذْ رُ كَا.

### p. 103, reading ذْ (sukun)
1. ذْ، ـذْ
2. أَذْ/كَا/رُ → أَذْكَارُ
3. Sight word: الْمَكْشُوفُ
4. Singing bird.

## Unit 9 summary for the app

- **New letters: ك، خ، ذ.** Total after Unit 9: 22.
- New activity ideas: dictation (spell a heard word with letter tiles), name the picture (write or build the word for a photo).
- The listening pictures partly reuse Part 1 stories (peacock, fox with a bell, grain mill, hyena's flute, train and shoe). These stories need texts once and serve both units.
- Values: respecting books, trust in God in danger, food hygiene.
