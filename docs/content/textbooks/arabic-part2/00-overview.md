## Important: Part 2 repeats Units 7 and 8

**Part 2 starts with Unit 7, not Unit 9.** Units 7 and 8 appear in both books. The story titles and letters are the same, but Part 2 is an **older trial edition** of these two units with:
- different story texts (shorter, with dialogue) and a different story picture,
- different sight words,
- extra page types: colouring the letter (palette symbol), write the missing letter, build the word from syllables,
- a speaking activity (mouth symbol) instead of some listening pictures.

For the app, Units 7 and 8 must be built **once**. The differences are recorded in `unit-07.md` and `unit-08.md` so the owner can choose which version to follow. Recommended: Part 1 (newer edition, 2017).

## Teaching approach (from المقدمة, p. 4)

- Continues Part 1: phonics entry (المدخل الصوتي), stand-alone and listening stories, letters ordered by frequency in children's speech.
- Completes the **remaining letters of the alphabet** and adds a unit on **language phenomena**: التضعيف (shadda)، التنوين، التاء المفتوحة والتاء المربوطة، اللام القمرية واللام الشمسية. Taught simply, linking sound and shape.
- Handwriting (الخط), dictation (الإملاء) and expression (التعبير) are introduced in simple form.
- Each lesson opens with a picture that expresses the meaning.
- Continuous assessment (exercises, tests) and final assessment.
- Companion: دليل المعلم (Part 2).

## Guidance for parents (p. 5)

The parent should: meet the teacher in the first two weeks of the term; learn the book's symbols; at home, listen to the child read the stand-alone story word by word, **listen when the child splits a word sound by sound and then reads it at once**, listen to the child read sight words as whole words, let the child retell the listening story and talk about the pictures; encourage writing with praise; follow the exercises at the end of each unit; watch for pronunciation difficulties; keep books and body clean; keep in contact with the teacher.
App relevance: this is the learning loop the app should support (sound-by-sound, then whole word; retelling; praise).

## Lesson structure in Part 2 (Units 7 and 8)

Each letter lesson has 10 pages:
1. Title page with the letter badge and a large picture (no text).
2. Story text page (fully printed, key letters in red).
3. **Colouring page** (palette symbol): the letter drawn as a large object (ح as a rope, س as a basket, ج as a bell), coloured model in class, outline for homework.
4. Reading page with fatha and long alif: ① syllable pills, ② split word → whole word (arrow boxes), ③ sight words, ④ listening picture.
5. Writing page: letter in its four forms (dotted to trace, empty to write) and four words; class and homework.
6. Reading page with damma and long و.
7. **أَنَا أَكْتُبُ الْحَرْفَ النَّاقِصَ**: write the missing letter in two words (class) and two words (homework).
8. Reading page with kasra and long ي.
9. **أَنَا أُرَكِّبُ**: build a word from three syllable bubbles; two sets in correct order, two scrambled.
10. Reading page with sukun.

App mapping: colouring → optional "colour the letter" (low priority); missing letter → `letter_pick` in a word gap; build the word → new "tap syllables in order" activity.
