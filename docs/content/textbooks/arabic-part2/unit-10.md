# Unit 10: الْوَحْدَةُ الْعَاشِرَةُ (new letters ع، ض، هـ)

Printed pp. 106 to 138 (PDF pp. 107 to 139). Three lessons, then تدريبات.

Unit cover (p. 106): an open book with: عَ، عَا، عِ، عِي، عُ، عُو، عْ، ضَ، ضَا (right page) and ضِ، ضِي، ضُ، ضُو، ضْ، هَ، هُ، هَا (left page).

## Lesson structure (changes from Unit 9)

Same 10 pages as Unit 9, with two upgrades:
- The writing page now traces **a whole sentence** under the four letter forms (instead of four words).
- Page 9 is **أَنَا أُرَكِّبُ جُمْلَةً** (build a sentence): three word cards in scrambled order; the child writes the sentence twice (class and homework).

App mapping: build a sentence → "tap the word cards in the right order" (sentence ordering).

## 10-1 شَاطِئُ عَدَنَ (letter ع), pp. 107 to 116

### p. 107, title page
Letter badge: ع. Picture: a crowded beach in Aden: waves, swimmers with rubber rings, red-brown volcanic mountains (a rock arch into the sea on the left), colourful sun umbrellas, white houses; in front, a man in a green shirt and shorts with a small boy shakes hands with a man in a yellow shirt and futa (the finder returning the wallet).

### p. 108, story text (fully printed)
- فِي عِيدِ الْفِطْرِ ذَهَبَتْ أُسْرَةُ عِمَادٍ إِلَى شَاطِئِ عَدَنَ.
- سَبَحَتِ الأُسْرَةُ فِي الْبَحْرِ، وَبَعْدَ سَاعَاتٍ ذَهَبَتْ إِلَى السُّوقِ، تَحَسَّسَ الْوَالِدُ جَيْبَ الْقَمِيصِ، وَقَالَ: يَا اللهُ ..! لَقَدْ أَضَعْتُ مِحْفَظَةَ النُّقُودِ فِي شَاطِئِ الْبَحْرِ يَا عُمَرُ.
- قَرَّرَتِ الأُسْرَةُ أَنْ تَعُودَ إِلَى الشَّاطِئِ، فَوَجَدَتْ رَجُلًا يُنَادِي: مَنْ فَقَدَ مِحْفَظَةً؟
- أَخَذَ الْوَالِدُ مِحْفَظَةَ النُّقُودِ، وَشَكَرَ الرَّجُلَ.

Red letters: عِيدِ، عِمَادٍ، عَدَنَ، بَعْدَ، سَاعَاتٍ، عُمَرُ، تَعُودَ.
Value: honesty (returning a lost wallet). Place: Aden beach, Eid al-Fitr.

### p. 109, colouring
The letter ع drawn on a clay pot tipped over, honey (عَسَل) pouring out, a bee on the pot. Coloured model and outline.

### p. 110, reading عَـ - عَا
1. عَـ، ـعَـ، ـعَ، عَ، عَا، ـعَا
2. عَـ/دَ/نُ → عَدَنُ ; سَا/عَا/تُ → سَاعَاتُ
3. Sight word: مِحْفَظَةٌ
4. Listening picture: two cats (grey and white) sit on either side of a monkey who holds a balance scale and bites a piece of cheese. The fable **القطتان والقرد** (the monkey "shares" the cheese fairly by eating it). Text not printed.

### p. 111, writing ع
Forms: عـ، ـعـ، ـع، ع. Sentence to trace: **علم بلادي مرفوع** (my country's flag is raised). Class and homework.

### p. 112, reading عُـ - عُو
1. عُـ، ـعُـ، ـعُ، عُ، عُو، ـعُو
2. عُـ/مَـ/رُ → عُمَرُ ; تَـ/عُو/دُ → تَعُودُ
3. Sight word: أَضَعْتُ
4. **Speaking picture**: Bab al-Yaman, the old gate of Sana'a, with tall tower houses behind; people walking through the gate, stalls, a car on the left.

### p. 113, dictation and picture naming
Class: dictation and write again (words not printed).
Homework picture names: عَسَل (honey jar with a dipper, solved example), عَيْن (an eye), عَلَم (the Yemeni flag).

### p. 114, reading عِـ - عِي
1. عِـ، ـعِـ، ـعِ، عِ، عِيـ، ـعِيـ، عِي، ـعِي
2. عِـ/مَا/دُ → عِمَادُ ; عِيـ/دُ → عِيدُ
3. Sight word: الشَّاطِئُ
4. Listening picture: a stork with a long beak drinks from a flat plate on a tree stump; a small yellow bird (or the fox's guest) watches. The fable **الثعلب واللقلق** (the fox serves soup on a flat plate, the stork serves it in a tall jar). Text not printed.

### p. 115, build a sentence
Class: العنب / عامر / يأكل → **عامر يأكل العنب** (written twice). Homework: عدن / أنا / أحب → **أنا أحب عدن**.

### p. 116, reading عْ (sukun)
1. عْـ، ـعْـ، ـعْ، عْ
2. بَعْـ/دُ → بَعْدُ
3. Sight word: تَحَسَّسَ
4. Singing bird.

## 10-2 فَضِيلَةُ وَالْحَاسُوبُ (letter ض), pp. 117 to 126

### p. 117, title page
Letter badge: ض. Picture: a girl in a white headscarf and pink dress sits at a desktop computer (a power symbol on the screen); a boy in a yellow shirt leans over and grabs the mouse, frowning; she looks up at him in protest.

### p. 118, story text (fully printed)
- كَانَتْ فَضِيلَةُ تَعْرِضُ الدُّرُوسَ فِي الْحَاسُوبِ، وَفِي يَوْمٍ بَدَأَتْ تَقْرَأُ مَوْضُوعَ ضَوْءِ الْقَمَرِ، تَضَايَقَ رِضْوَانُ، وَأَغْلَقَ الْحَاسُوبَ.
- قَالَتْ فَضِيلَةُ: لِمَاذَا؟!
- قَالَ رِضْوَانُ: لَقَدْ ضِقْتُ، فَأَنَا أُرِيدُ أَنْ أَسْتَخْدِمَ الْحَاسُوبَ.
- سَمِعَتِ الأُمُّ الْحَدِيثَ، وَقَالَتْ: تَعَالَوْا نَتَّفِقُ عَلَى وَقْتِ اسْتِخْدَامِ الْحَاسُوبِ، وَحَدَّدَتْ وَقْتًا لِفَضِيلَةَ وَوَقْتًا لِرِضْوَانَ.

Red letters: تَعْرِضُ، مَوْضُوعَ، ضَوْءِ، تَضَايَقَ، رِضْوَانُ، فَضِيلَةُ، ضِقْتُ.
Value: sharing, taking turns, solving conflicts by agreement.

### p. 119, colouring
The letter ض drawn on the back of a frog (ضِفْدَع) sitting on a lily pad in a pond. Coloured model and outline.

### p. 120, reading ضَـ - ضَا
1. ضَـ، ـضَـ، ـضَ، ضَ، ضَا، ـضَا
2. ضَوْ/ءُ → ضَوْءُ ; تَـ/ضَا/يَـ/قَ → تَضَايَقَ
3. Sight word: الْحَاسُوبُ
4. Listening picture: two dogs fight each other on a rock (barking, angry) while a wolf carries off a lamb from the flock in the valley behind. Fable (quarrelling lets the enemy win). Text not printed.

### p. 121, writing ض
Forms: ضـ، ـضـ، ـض، ض. Sentence: **ضوء القمر جميل**. Class and homework.

### p. 122, reading ضُـ - ضُو
1. ضُـ، ـضُـ، ـضُ، ضُ، ضُو، ـضُو
2. تَعْـ/رِ/ضُ → تَعْرِضُ ; مَوْ/ضُو/عُ → مَوْضُوعُ
3. Sight word: الْحَدِيثُ
4. **Speaking picture**: Eid celebration in a park: a man in a futa and jambiya greets a boy; boys shake hands and hold balloons; girls fly a kite; lanterns and fireworks.

### p. 123, dictation and picture naming
Class: dictation and write again.
Homework picture names: ضَبُع (a hyena, solved example), ضَرْس / أَضْرَاس (molar teeth), ضَابِط (a police officer in uniform).

### p. 124, reading ضِـ - ضِي
1. ضِـ، ـضِـ، ـضِ، ضِ، ضِيـ، ـضِيـ، ضِي، ـضِي
2. ضِقْ/تُ → ضِقْتُ ; فَـ/ضِيـ/لَـ/ةُ → فَضِيلَةُ
3. Sight word: أَغْلَقَ
4. Listening picture: a girl in a pink headscarf sits on a mat outside her house reading a book; a white goat eats from her tray of tea glasses and teapot. Text not printed.

### p. 125, build a sentence
Class: الدروس / فضيلة / تعرض → **فضيلة تعرض الدروس**. Homework: القراءة / يحب / رضوان → **رضوان يحب القراءة**.

### p. 126, reading ضْ (sukun)
1. ضْـ، ـضْـ، ـضْ، ضْ
2. رِضْ/وَا/نُ → رِضْوَانُ
3. Sight word: حَدَّدَتْ
4. Singing bird.

## 10-3 هِلَالٌ وَالْعَسَلُ (letter هـ), pp. 127 to 136

### p. 127, title page
Letter badge: هـ. Picture: a boy in a blue jacket holds his forehead in pain beside three log beehives on a stand in a meadow; an angry big bee and a smaller bee fly near the hives; a tree and flowers.

### p. 128, story text (fully printed)
- شَاهَدَ هِلَالٌ خَلِيَّةَ نَحْلٍ، فَقَالَ: كَيْفَ تَصْنَعُ النَّحْلُ الْعَسَلَ يَا وَهِيبَةُ؟ قَالَتْ: تَجْمَعُ رَحِيقَ أَزْهَارِ الأَشْجَارِ، فَيَتَحَوَّلُ - بِإِذْنِ اللهِ - إِلَى عَسَلٍ لَذِيذٍ فِي شُهُورٍ قَلِيلَةٍ.
- مَدَّ هِلَالٌ يَدَهُ إِلَى الْخَلِيَّةِ فِي هُدُوءٍ، فَلَسَعَتْهُ نَحْلَةٌ.
- قَالَتْ وَهِيبَةُ: ابْتَعِدْ يَا هِلَالُ، فَأَخْذُ الْعَسَلِ مِنَ الْخَلِيَّةِ لَيْسَ سَهْلًا.

Red letters: شَاهَدَ، وَهِيبَةُ، أَزْهَارِ، شُهُورٍ، هِلَالٌ، هُدُوءٍ، سَهْلًا.
Value: knowledge about bees and honey (Yemeni honey is famous), caution.

### p. 129, colouring
The letter هـ drawn as the eye and crest of a hoopoe (هُدْهُد) perched on a branch. Coloured model and outline.

### p. 130, reading هَـ - هَا
1. هَـ، ـهَـ، ـهَ، هَ، هَا، ـهَا
2. شَا/هَـ/دَ → شَاهَدَ ; أَزْ/هَا/رُ → أَزْهَارُ
3. Sight words: لَذِيذٌ، تَصْنَعُ
4. Listening picture: three men in turbans and old-style robes stand around a stone well with a wooden frame; one in yellow robes points to the well, another in white listens, the third leans on the well. A traditional tale (possibly Juha and the well, or judging between neighbours). Text not printed.

### p. 131, writing هـ
Forms: هـ، ـهـ، ـه، ه. Sentence: **هلال يحب الأزهار الجميلة**. Class and homework.

### p. 132, reading هُـ - هُو
1. هُـ، ـهُـ، ـهُ، هُ، هُو، ـهُو
2. هُـ/دُو/ءُ → هُدُوءُ ; شُـ/هُو/رُ → شُهُورُ
3. Sight words: خَلِيَّةٌ، رَحِيقٌ
4. **Speaking picture**: a waterfall and stone bridges in a rocky valley with stairs (a Yemeni tourist place such as a dam or a wadi waterfall); people on the bridges and the bank.

### p. 133, dictation and picture naming
Class: dictation and write again.
Homework picture names: نَهْر (a river, solved example), هُدْهُد (a hoopoe), هَاتِف (a telephone).

### p. 134, reading هِـ - هِي
1. هِـ، ـهِـ، ـهِ، هِ، هِيـ، ـهِيـ، هِي، ـهِي
2. وَ/هِيـ/بَـ/ةُ → وَهِيبَةُ ; هِـ/لَا/لُ → هِلَالُ
3. Sight word: يَتَحَوَّلُ
4. Listening picture: a tiger looks into a pond and sees its reflection; a rabbit peeks from the bushes behind. The fable of **the lion (tiger) and the clever rabbit** who tricks it into fighting its own reflection. Text not printed.

### p. 135, build a sentence
Class: وطني / أنا / أهوى → **أنا أهوى وطني**. Homework: الأزهار / وهيبة / تحب → **وهيبة تحب الأزهار**.

### p. 136, reading هْ (sukun)
1. هْـ، ـهْـ، ـهْ، هْ
2. سَهْـ/لُ → سَهْلُ
3. Sight word: لَسَعَتْهُ
4. Singing bird.

## Unit 10 summary for the app

- **New letters: ع، ض، هـ.** Total after Unit 10: 25.
- New skills: tracing a whole sentence; building a sentence from three word cards.
- Yemeni places and culture: Aden beach, Bab al-Yaman (Sana'a), a waterfall and bridges, Eid, the jambiya, Yemeni honey, the hoopoe.
- Listening stories needing texts: the monkey and the two cats, the fox and the stork, the two dogs and the wolf, the girl and the goat, the three men at the well, the tiger and the rabbit.
- Values: honesty, sharing the computer, caution with bees.
