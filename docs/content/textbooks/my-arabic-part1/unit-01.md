# Unit 1: الْوَحْدَةُ الأُولَى (pre-reading)

Printed pp. 7 to 27 (PDF pp. 9 to 29). Eleven short lessons, mostly pictures. Every lesson opens with **مُخْرَجَاتُ الدَّرْسِ** (learning outcomes) and some pages end with a teacher note **لِلْمُعَلِّمِ وَالْمُعَلِّمَةِ**.

## Unit outcomes (p. 7)
By the end of the unit the child can: (1) recognise pictures of things around them and say their names; (2) identify the sounds of things they hear; (3) tell things apart by size, colour and number; (4) link related everyday things; (5) tell same from different shapes; (6) trace shapes and lines with a pencil; (7) describe a given picture orally.

## Lesson 1: تَعَرُّفُ الأَشْيَاءِ (pp. 8 to 9)
- **أتعرف الصورة** (p. 8): a photo of a Quran (dark blue cover with gold decoration). Word: الْمُصْحَف / الْقُرْآن.
- **أسمي الصور الآتية** (p. 9): four pictures: a tree on a green hill (شَجَرَة); a pink Yemeni house with coloured arched (qamariya) windows and a wooden door (بَيْت); a smartphone (هَاتِف / جَوَّال); the Al-Saleh mosque in Sana'a with tall minarets and white domes (مَسْجِد).
App: `naming`.

## Lesson 2: تَمْيِيزُ صُوَرِ الأَشْيَاءِ وَأَصْوَاتِهَا (p. 10)
**أسمي**: six photos: Yemenia aircraft (طَائِرَة), a white hen (دَجَاجَة), a wall clock (سَاعَة), a cow (بَقَرَة), an orange cat (قِطَّة), a red whistle (صَفَّارَة).
Teacher note: point to the picture and ask "مَا هَذِهِ الصُّورَةُ؟"; **use a sound-recognition programme (on a phone or computer) if available** to play each thing's sound.
App: `naming` + `listen_pick` with real sounds (the book itself suggests digital audio).

## Lesson 3: التَّمْيِيزُ الْبَصَرِيُّ (pp. 11 to 12)
- **أصل بخط بين كل صورتين متماثلتين** (p. 11): match identical pairs: red car, red apple, the Seiyun palace (white tower palace), in two shuffled columns.
- **أضع دائرة حول الصورة ذات اللون المختلف** (p. 12): row 1: red apple, red apple, **green apple**, red apple; row 2: football, football, **basketball**, football.
- **أضع دائرة حول الصورة المماثلة للصورة الأولى** (p. 12): row 1: model cat, then donkey, dog, **cat**; row 2: model pencil, then eraser, **pencil**, book.
App: `matching` (identical pairs), odd one out (different colour), find the same as the model.

## Lesson 4: الْفَرْقُ بَيْنَ الأَشْيَاءِ (p. 13)
**ألاحظ**: three pairs. (1) size: a long and a short stick; a cow and a calf (كَبِير / صَغِير). (2) colour: a red and a green apple; a red and a blue pen. (3) number: 2 oranges and 3 oranges; 2 birds and 4 birds.
Teacher note: name the size, help the child name the colour, say the number.
App: new "which is bigger / which colour / how many" questions.

## Lesson 5: الأَوْضَاعُ الْمَكَانِيَّةُ (p. 14)
**أحدد مكان الأشياء في الآتي**: six pictures: a cat **under** a chair (تَحْتَ); a man **on top of** a house (فَوْقَ); books **inside** a school bag (دَاخِلَ); two girls **at** a desk (أَمَامَ / خَلْفَ); a boy walking **in front of** a house; a car **in front of** a school, a tree **beside** it (بِجَانِبِ).
Teacher note: "أين القط؟ تحت الكرسي. أين الرجل؟ فوق البيت."
App: `listen_pick` "where is the cat?" with position words.

## Lesson 6: تَتَبُّعُ الأَشْكَالِ وَالرَّسْمِ (p. 15)
**أصل بين النقط لرسم الأشكال**: (1) a zigzag line (dotted, with direction arrows, starting from the right); (2) a square, a circle and a triangle (dotted, arrows); (3) a bicycle with one dotted wheel to complete.
App: `trace` (lines and shapes).

## Lesson 7: فِي الْمَدْرَسَةِ (pp. 16 to 18)
- **أتعرف الصورة** (p. 16): a schoolyard; the Yemeni flag on a pole; a teacher in a white robe welcomes children; a mother in a black abaya and a father in a white thobe, jacket and head cloth bring two children with school bags.
- **أصف الصور الآتية** (p. 17): four scenes: two boys in green uniforms shaking hands in the courtyard; a teacher in a brown jacket at the blackboard, a pupil at the door; boys sitting on a bench in the courtyard; a man in a dark jacket (the head teacher) talking to two pupils.
- **أجد هذه الأشياء في مدرستي** (p. 18): a desk (طَاوِلَة / مَقْعَد), a chair (كُرْسِيّ), a school bag (حَقِيبَة), a blackboard with writing (سَبُّورَة), a sharpener (مِبْرَاة), a pencil (قَلَم).
Values: greeting, respect for teachers.

## Lesson 8: فِي الْبَيْتِ (pp. 19 to 20)
- **أتعرف الصورة** (p. 19): a pink Yemeni house with coloured qamariya windows and a wooden door, trees around.
- **أصف الصور الآتية** (p. 20): the Quran; a qamariya (semicircular stained-glass window: blue, orange, green, red); a kitchen (cooker, gas cylinder, sink, cupboards, red curtain); a mafraj sitting room (floor cushions, red curtains, arched windows).

## Lesson 9: التَّعْبِيرُ بِالصُّورَةِ (pp. 21 to 22)
- **أعبر عن الصور شفهيًا** (p. 21): four numbered pictures forming a sequence: (1) a boy packs his school bag at home; (2) he walks to school on the pavement; (3) the schoolyard with the Yemeni flag and pupils; (4) pupils writing at their desks.
- **أرتب الصور الآتية لتكوين قصة** (p. 22): four pictures in mixed order: a girl in a white headscarf and orange dress walks with a blue bucket; she opens the door of a chicken coop, hens come out; she feeds the hens and chicks grain; her smiling face. Correct order: walk with bucket → open the coop → feed the hens → happy.
App: new activity "order the pictures to make a story" (sequencing).

## Lesson 10: هَيَّا نُنْشِدُ (p. 23)
Picture: boys in green uniforms standing in rows in the schoolyard (morning assembly). Song (poet سليمان العيسى، ديوان الأطفال):
> ١- أَهْلًا أَهْلًا ** يَا مَدْرَسَتِي
> ٢- هَيَّا نَقْرَأْ ** أَحْلَى لُغَةِ
> ٣- نَكْبُرُ مَعَهَا ** نَحْلُو مَعَهَا
> ٤- لُغَتِي الْفُصْحَى ** مَا أَرْوَعَهَا!

App: audio song.

## Lesson 11: تَقْوِيمُ الْوَحْدَةِ الأُولَى (pp. 24 to 27)
1. **سَمِّ الصُّوَرَ الآتِيَةَ** (p. 24): dog, tree, a Sana'a tower house, hen, bicycle, chair.
2. **صِلْ بَيْنَ الأَشْيَاءِ عَلَى نَمَطِ الْمِثَالِ** (p. 25): link related things: board eraser → blackboard (printed example), school bag → notebook, key → padlock, cooking pot → pot lid.
3. **ضَعْ دَائِرَةً حَوْلَ الصُّورَةِ الْمُخْتَلِفَةِ** (p. 25): blue books with one **red book**; three orange paper planes and one **small white paper plane**; four tables with books, one has **one book** instead of a pile.
4. **صِلْ بَيْنَ كُلِّ صُورَتَيْنِ مُتَمَاثِلَتَيْنِ** (p. 26): tree, donkey, apple pairs.
5. **اذْكُرْ أَسْمَاءَ الصُّوَرِ وَلَوِّنْهَا** (p. 26): outline drawings of a rose, a car and a cat to name and colour.
6. **لَوِّنْ عَلَمَ بِلَادِي** (p. 27): outline of a waving flag with three stripes to colour (red, white, black).
