## Teaching approach (from مقدمة, p. 4)

1. Letters and target words are linked to pictures ("القراءة المصورة").
2. **Each letter is taught with its short and long vowels** (الصوائت القصيرة والطويلة), and the book is built letter by letter **in alphabetical order (from أ to the end of the alphabet)**.
3. Lesson vocabulary serves the lesson title and comes from the child's familiar environment.
4. Writing skills (expression and handwriting) follow a sequence: words, then sentences, then simple structures.
5. Lesson topics are chosen to fit the letter; the entry is the letter with its vowels.
6. Part 2's last unit adds sound phenomena: التنوين، الألف المقصورة، التاء المربوطة، الهاء، ال الشمسية وال القمرية.
7. Simple sentences, then compound sentences, to build a short text.
8. Some faith concepts suited to the age, and principles and identity of the Yemeni people.

## Key difference from أقرأ وأتعلم

| | لغتي العربية (Sana'a 2026) | أقرأ وأتعلم (Aden 2017) |
|---|---|---|
| Letter order | alphabetical (أ ب ت ث ج ح خ ...) | by frequency (م ب ل ن ر د ت ...) |
| One lesson = | one letter with all vowels (6 pages) | one story + one letter, vowels spread over units |
| Letters in Part 1 | 19: أ to غ | 19: م ب ل ن ر د ت ي و أ ق ص ف ح س ج ش ط ز |
| Unit 1 | 11 short pre-reading lessons | 5 pre-reading lessons |
| Songs | one نشيد per unit | listening pictures |

The current app's Arabic units (alphabetical: ب ت ث / ج ح خ / ...) are much closer to **this** book than to أقرأ وأتعلم.

## For the app (course `lughati`, book `lughati-1`)

- **Fits the app's current activity types well.** Each letter lesson maps almost one to one:
  - scene picture → `story` (picture talk, no text)
  - short and long vowels with three key words each → `naming` + `listen_pick` (hear the syllable, pick the picture)
  - "which pictures contain the letter?" → new "find the sound" activity (6 pictures, tap those with the letter)
  - circle the letter in words → `letter_pick` inside a word
  - match identical words → `matching`
  - trace the letter with fatha, kasra, damma → `trace`
  - copy words → `trace` of whole words (or skip on a phone)
- **No reading stories** in Part 1, only scene pictures to talk about and about 15 to 20 picture words per letter. Story texts are not needed; the audio is mostly single words and syllables, which makes recording simpler than for أقرأ وأتعلم.
- Four songs to record: أهلا يا مدرستي، أمي وأبي، وطني، مدرستي.
- Every unit test is a ready quiz bank (same word, word to picture, letter form, first sound with vowel, "which word contains the letter", missing letter from a picture).
- About 19 letters × 6 pages + 11 pre-reading lessons + 3 tests ≈ 35 app lessons for Part 1.
- Known misprints to tell the teacher: wrong letter named in some instruction headers (pp. 87, 103, 105, 111), مِظَلَّة / مَظَلَّة spelling (pp. 141, 142), the poet's name hidden on p. 71.
