# Unit 5: الأَشْكَالُ الْهَنْدَسِيَّةُ وَالْكُسُورُ (shapes and fractions)

Printed pp. 117 to 139 (PDF pp. 119 to 141). Eight lessons, then unit test, reteaching and enrichment.

## Unit opener (p. 117)
Space background with a ringed planet; floating solids (cone, cylinder, sphere cut in quarters, cuboid, triangular prism, pentagonal prisms, a square cut in half) and a smiling green pyramid character.

---

## Lesson 5-1: الْمُجَسَّمَاتُ (solids) (pp. 118 to 121)
- **أتعلم: مَنْ أَنَا؟** (p. 118): smiling solids introduce themselves in speech bubbles: **أنا متوازي المستطيلات** (cuboid), **أنا الإسطوانة** (cylinder), **أنا الكرة** (sphere), **أنا المكعب** (cube), **أنا الهرم** (pyramid), **أنا المخروط** (cone, a party hat).
- **أتدرب** (pp. 119 to 120): ring the everyday object with the same shape as the model: cube → **dice**; cylinder → **tin can**; cone → **party hat** / ice-cream cone; cuboid → box of butter / a sponge; sphere → ball / orange; join each solid to its name (هرم، كرة، متوازي مستطيلات، مخروط، إسطوانة، مكعب); count the solids of each kind in a pile and fill a table.
- **أفكر** (p. 121): eight cubes: can you build a cuboid from them? a cube? (نعم / لا), check with real cubes.

## Lesson 5-2: الأَشْكَالُ الْمُسْتَوِيَةُ (flat shapes) (pp. 122 to 125)
- **أتعلم** (p. 122): everyday objects joined to shapes: a wooden door → rectangle, a framed mirror → square, a **"school children" road sign** → triangle, a red ring → circle; shape characters **مربع، مستطيل، دائرة، مثلث**.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 123 to 125): join matching shapes; join each shape to its name; colour shapes of the same kind with one colour; count circles, triangles, rectangles and squares in a mixed picture and fill a table (example squares 3); colour rectangles green, squares blue, circles yellow, triangles red.

## Lesson 5-3: اكْتِشَافُ الأَنْمَاطِ (pp. 126 to 127)
Ring the solid that continues the pattern (ball, cube…; pyramid, cube…; two pyramids, cylinder…); complete flat-shape patterns (two green triangles and a red one; rectangle, circle, triangle); ring the next tile (circle in a square / square in a circle).

## Lesson 5-4: حَلُّ الْمَسْأَلَةِ (pp. 128 to 129)
- **أفكر** (p. 128): colour a picture made of shapes by the key (rectangles yellow, triangles green, circles red): a sailing boat with portholes on the sea, mountains, fish and seaweed.
- (p. 129): a **tangram animal** (a fox or dog) made of 7 numbered pieces: write the numbers on the loose pieces and colour them like the model.

## Lesson 5-5: النِّصْفُ (half) (pp. 130 to 131)
- **أتعلم** (p. 130): an orange and an apple cut into two equal halves; a circle cut in two: **جزء من جزأين متساويين هو ١/٢**.
- **أتدرب**: colour half of a house shape, circle, triangle, rectangle; ring the shapes coloured in half (some are cut unequally); **أتحدث**: "Ahmad says the coloured part of these shapes is a half. How can you explain his mistake?" (unequal parts); **أفكر**: complete a half-coloured circle pattern.

## Lesson 5-6: الرُّبْعُ (quarter) (pp. 132 to 133)
- **أتعلم** (p. 132): an apple cut into four equal pieces; a square in four colours: **جزء من أربعة أجزاء متساوية هو ربع ١/٤**.
- **أتدرب**: colour a quarter of shapes; ring the shapes with a quarter coloured (a triangle split into 4 is a trap: middle piece); choose ١/٢ or ١/٤ for each shape; **أتحدث**: what is the difference between ١/٢ and ١/٤?; **أفكر**: a circle pattern filling up in quarters.

## Lesson 5-7: تَدْرِيبَاتٌ عَامَّةٌ (pp. 134 to 135)
Ring ١/٢ or ١/٤ for coloured shapes; colour one equal part and ring its fraction; join coloured shapes to ١/٤ and ١/٢; **أفكر**: which fraction is missing from the circle (a quarter missing from a three-quarter disc); **أحل المسألة**: **"أكل أحمد ١/٢ تفاحة وأكلت أخته فاطمة ١/٢ التفاحة الأخرى. من أكل أكثر؟"** (neither: equal halves).

## Lesson 5-8: هَيَّا بِنَا نَلْعَبُ وَنَتَعَلَّمُ (let's play and learn) (p. 136)
A schoolyard game: a PE teacher in a yellow shirt with boys in orange numbered kit (1 to 10); shapes and fractions drawn on the ground; each boy jumps to the shape the teacher names. Played by the rules in the teacher's guide.

## اختبار الوحدة (p. 137)
Join solids to names (مكعب، كرة، إسطوانة); colour rectangles red, triangles blue, circles yellow in a picture of a car made of shapes; ring the fraction coloured (١/٤ of a bar, ١/٢ of a rhombus).

## إعادة التعليم (p. 138)
Colour half of a circle, triangle, rectangle; colour a quarter of three shapes; colour the cylinders red, cubes yellow and the sphere green.

## إثراء (p. 139)
Join matching shape robots; ring the fraction coloured on bars of 8 parts (2 of 8 = ١/٤, 4 of 8 = ١/٢); continue a quarter-circle pattern.

## Unit 5 summary for the app
- The app's current Math Unit 3 (shapes: naming, matching objects to shapes, listen and pick) covers part of lessons 5-1 and 5-2. Missing: the six solids and their names, counting shapes in a picture, patterns with shapes, half and quarter.
- New question types: "which object has this shape?" (dice → cube); "is the coloured part a half?" (yes / no, with unequal-part traps); choose ١/٢ or ١/٤; tangram.
- Vocabulary to record: مُكَعَّب، كُرَة، إِسْطُوَانَة، مَخْرُوط، هَرَم، مُتَوَازِي الْمُسْتَطِيلَات، مُرَبَّع، مُسْتَطِيل، دَائِرَة، مُثَلَّث، نِصْف، رُبْع.
