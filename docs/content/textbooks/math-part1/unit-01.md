# Unit 1: التَّصْنِيفُ وَالأَنْمَاطُ (classification and patterns)

Printed pp. 7 to 32 (PDF pp. 9 to 34). Seven lessons, then اختبار الوحدة (unit test), إعادة التعليم (reteaching) and إثراء (enrichment). Almost no text for the child: everything is pictures; the instructions are in the teacher box **لِلْمُعَلِّمِ وَالْمُعَلِّمَةِ** at the bottom of each page. Section icons: **أَتَعَلَّمُ** (boy with a book: worked example), **أَتَدَرَّبُ** (girl in a headscarf writing: practice), **أُفَكِّرُ** (thinking child: challenge).

Answer marks used throughout: tick (✓) under a picture, draw a line (وصل) between pictures, ring (يحوط) a group, colour (يلون), draw (يرسم).

## Unit opener (p. 7)
A Yemeni family outing in a sandy landscape with dunes: two **date palms** with bunches of dates; a boy climbs a palm with a climbing belt and lowers a basket on a rope to another boy; a pond with two ducks, a third duck outside; four children run with balloons (blue, red, blue, red), a girl in a white headscarf; a family on a yellow mat: father in a white shirt, mother in a headscarf, two children, a plate of food, a teapot and cups. The picture reappears in later questions (count, more / less, inside / outside, patterns).

## Lesson 1-1: التَّصْنِيفُ وَفْقَ خَاصِيَّةٍ (one property) (pp. 8 to 11)
- **أتعلم** (p. 8): school things in a column (ruler, maths book "الرياضيات", eraser, pencil, notebook, sharpener, science book "العلوم", box of crayons); a small red pencil case and a big green school bag. Teacher: link the small things to the small case and the big things to the big bag (size).
- **أتدرب** (p. 9): (1) four footballs of different sizes, tick the one the same size as the model; (2) pink hats big and small, join each pair of the same size; (3) ring the group with the same size (cans, mugs).
- **أتدرب** (p. 10): by colour: model red apple, choose among green, red (✓ example), yellow apples; (1) model yellow flower among red, green, yellow; (2) model red sharpener among red and blue; (3) ring the T-shirts of the same colour (green, orange ×3, blue).
- **أفكر** (p. 11): by shape: (1) tick the odd shape (two circles and a hexagon); (2) join identical shapes (triangle, square, ellipse); (3) ring the shapes that are the same (circle, pentagon, three rhombuses).

## Lesson 1-2: التَّصْنِيفُ وَفْقَ خَاصِيَّتَيْنِ (two properties) (pp. 12 to 13)
- **أتعلم** (p. 12): yellow triangles, pink circles and blue squares joined to the matching shape-and-colour model.
- **أتدرب** (p. 12): (1) clocks: tick those with the same shape and size; (2) put shapes with the same shape and colour in the big box, the others in the small box; (3) ring shapes with the same shape and colour.
- **أفكر** (p. 13): (1) plates: tick the one with the same colour and size as the model; (2) ring groups of identical items (balls, fish, hens); (3) tick the boots with the same size and colour; (4) ring shapes with the same shape and colour.

## Lesson 1-3: أَكْثَرُ مِنْ - أَقَلُّ مِنْ - يُسَاوِي (more, fewer, equal) (pp. 14 to 17)
- **أتعلم** (p. 14): roses and bees joined one to one: 5 roses and 4 bees → أكثر من; 4 roses and 3 bees → أقل من (read from the bees' side); 4 and 4 → يساوي.
- **أتدرب** (p. 14): (1) children and chairs; (2) rulers and pencils; (3) rabbits and carrots: join one to one, ring the smaller / bigger group, say يساوي.
- **More practice** (pp. 15 to 16): toothbrushes and toothpaste (4 and 4); 5 shirts and 4 trousers; 3 pens and 4 notebooks; 4 cups and 4 saucers; 4 shoes and 6 socks; 4 girls in headscarves and 4 handbags.
- **أفكر** (p. 17): colour flowers red and yellow so that red > yellow; draw circles equal to 5 triangles; draw a group more than 3 squares; draw a group fewer than 4 apples.

## Lesson 1-4: الْمَوَاقِعُ النِّسْبِيَّةُ (position) (pp. 18 to 21)
### فَوْقَ - تَحْتَ (pp. 18 to 19)
- **أتعلم**: a bird on a branch (فوق) and a fox under a tree (تحت).
- **أتدرب**: tick the ball under the chair; tick the cube on the table; join the words to a boy lying under a tree and a bird above; (4) stacked boxes red / white / black (the Yemeni flag colours): name the box above and below the white one; (5) colour the balloon under the bench blue; (6) a stack of books: name the colour above the green one and below the blue one.
### دَاخِلَ - خَارِجَ (pp. 20 to 21)
- **أتعلم**: ducks inside a pond, one duck outside.
- **أتدرب**: tick the ball inside the goal; colour the rooster outside the cage; say what is inside a basket and ring what is outside it; (4) colour the flowers outside a vase yellow; (5) tick the boy inside the bus; (6) draw a fence so that more hens are inside than outside.

## Lesson 1-5: اكْتِشَافُ الأَنْمَاطِ (patterns) (pp. 22 to 25)
- **أتعلم** (p. 22): frog, turtle, frog, turtle…; hen, two chicks, hen…; cow, calf, cow, calf… Teacher: let the child describe the pattern.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 23 to 25): ring the next item (tall / short candles; bee / two flowers; two kinds of leaves); colour to continue (apples yellow / red; half-coloured circles; shapes circle / triangle / square); **letters as a pattern**: م أ م أ … (ring the next letter); continue circle-in-ring patterns; draw the missing shapes.

## Lesson 1-6: حَلُّ الْمَسْأَلَةِ (problem solving) (pp. 26 to 27)
(1) Draw the next step to build a house shape from lines; (2) tick the shapes with the same shape and colour combination; (3) draw the missing shape and colour it; (4) complete a square / circle colour pattern; (5) colour six balloons in two colours to make a pattern; (6) complete a grid pattern (homework).

## Lesson 1-7: تَدْرِيبَاتٌ عَامَّةٌ (mixed practice) (pp. 28 to 29)
Join shapes to a big or small box by colour and shape; ring shapes of the same shape and colour; tick rectangles of the same size and colour; tick dresses of the same colour; tick the group that has more than the boys; continue a shape pattern.

## اختبار الوحدة (unit test, p. 30)
(1) Ring shapes of the same shape and colour; (2) tick the bigger group (5 apples, 5 boys); (3) join the vase and the ball to فوق / تحت a table; (4) tick the bird inside the cage; (5) draw the next two shapes of a triangle / rhombus pattern.

## إعادة التعليم (reteaching, p. 31)
Tick the odd one out (a car among bicycles); join shapes by colour; complete a pattern; join the pencils to the bigger group.

## إثراء (enrichment, p. 32)
Draw a group more than and one fewer than 5 circles; join identical shapes; complete a happy / sad face pattern; continue a pattern with three more shapes.

## Unit 1 summary for the app
- The current app has nothing equivalent; Math Unit 1 in the app starts with counting. This unit is pre-number and needs picture-only activities:
  - **odd one out / same as the model** (size, colour, shape): a picture `letter_pick`-style choice with images.
  - **sort into two boxes** (big and small, by colour and shape): a new drag or tap-to-sort activity.
  - **one-to-one matching** to compare groups: `matching`.
  - **more / fewer / equal**: choose between two groups, or answer with three word buttons.
  - **position words** (فوق، تحت، داخل، خارج): `listen_pick` with scene pictures.
  - **patterns**: "what comes next?" with 3 picture choices (fits `letter_pick` with images, including the م أ letter pattern).
- Yemeni scenes: date palms with a climber and basket, a family picnic on a mat with a teapot, the flag colours as stacked boxes.
- All instructions are for the teacher; the app must speak them (audio) because the child cannot read yet.
