## Approach (مقدمة, p. 4)
The book was rewritten for Grade 1 with: close links between maths and everyday situations; varied, attractive presentation; problem solving and discovering patterns; varied assessment matched to learning outcomes. A teacher's guide (دليل المعلم) accompanies it.

## Page conventions
- Section icons: **أَتَعَلَّمُ** (worked example), **أَتَدَرَّبُ** (practice), **أُفَكِّرُ** (challenge), **أَقْرَأُ وَأَكْتُبُ** (read and write digits), **أَتَحَدَّثُ** (talk about it).
- Every page ends with a **لِلْمُعَلِّمِ وَالْمُعَلِّمَةِ** box: the child cannot read yet, so all instructions are for the teacher.
- Each unit ends with **اختبار الوحدة** (test), **إعادة التعليم** (reteaching) and **إثراء** (enrichment).
- Numerals are Eastern Arabic (٠ to ٩); sums and comparisons are printed right to left.

The app's order (counting → arithmetic → shapes) follows the book, but it has 11 lessons where the book has 41. A rebuild along the book, like Arabic Unit 2, would give about 5 app units and 40 to 50 lessons.

## Activities the app would need
Existing types cover a good part: `counting`, `arithmetic`, `listen_pick`, `naming`, `matching`, `trace` (digits). New or extended:
1. **Picture choice with images** (odd one out, same as the model, which object has this shape, next in the pattern).
2. **Compare** two numbers or groups with three buttons (أكثر / أقل / يساوي or < > =), printed RTL.
3. **Order** three numbers; **ordinal** "which one is 4th?" on a row of pictures.
4. **Cross out** to subtract (tap objects to cross them out, then pick the result).
5. **Missing number** in a sum or difference.
6. **Fraction**: is the coloured part a half? choose ١/٢ or ١/٤.
7. **Sort** into two boxes (by size, colour or shape).
