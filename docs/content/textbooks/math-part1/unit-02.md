# Unit 2: الأَعْدَادُ (٠ - ١٠) (numbers 0 to 10)

Printed pp. 33 to 64 (PDF pp. 35 to 66). Ten lessons, then unit test, reteaching and enrichment. Numerals are **Eastern Arabic digits** (٠ ١ ٢ ٣ ٤ ٥ ٦ ٧ ٨ ٩ ١٠) throughout, with number words under the worked examples. Each number lesson has **أتعلم** (picture groups with digit and word), **أقرأ وأكتب** (trace the digit on dots, then a pink table: one model digit and seven boxes per row to copy), **أتدرب** and **أفكر**.

## Unit opener (p. 33)
Four boys in a park play football under a big tree; six yellow chicks sit on a branch; a hen with three chicks on the grass; flowers. Used for counting.

## Lesson 2-1: الأَعْدَادُ ١، ٢، ٣ (pp. 34 to 37)
- **أتعلم** (p. 34): 1, 2, 3 birds with ١ واحد، ٢ اثنان، ٣ ثلاثة.
- **أتدرب** (p. 34): shade a bar of three boxes for 1, 2, 3 fish; join groups of 2 girls, 1 girl, 3 boys to the numerals.
- (p. 35): count balls and ring the number (example 3); join groups (books, pens, erasers, pencils, rulers, a sharpener) to ١ ٢ ٣; **أفكر**: count the letters ق، ل، ب، م scattered on the page and shade bars.
- **أقرأ وأكتب** (pp. 36 to 37): candles 1, 2, 3; trace ١ (balloon, glasses, parrot) and ٢ (babies, bees, watches) and ٣ (rabbits, cats, hens) in tables; **أفكر**: draw lines to show 2, 3, 1; count the jug, cups and glasses on a table and write the number.

## Lesson 2-2: الأَعْدَادُ ٤، ٥، ٠ (pp. 38 to 41)
- **أتعلم** (p. 38): fishbowls with 4 fish, 5 fish and an empty bowl: ٤ أربعة، ٥ خمسة، ٠ صفر.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 38 to 39): fenced fields with 5 cows, 4 cows, none: join to ٥ ٤ ٠; **girls in green uniforms and white headscarves holding small Yemeni flags** (4, none, 5): ring the number; rabbit cages (4, 5, empty): tick the number; **أفكر**: join vases with flowers and baskets with fish to ٤ ٥ ٠.
- **أقرأ وأكتب** (pp. 40 to 41): trays with 4 glasses, 5 glasses, none; trace ٤ (birds, chicks, pigeons) and ٥ (bees, butterflies, locusts); complete a vase table for ٠ ٤ ٥; count oranges on plates (5, 0, 4); **أفكر**: a family at a dining table (two girls in headscarves, two boys) with plates, a frying pan and a teapot: count spoons, glasses, cups, plates.

## Lesson 2-3: الأَعْدَادُ ٦، ٧، ٨ (pp. 42 to 45)
- **أتعلم** (p. 42): 6 basketballs ٦ ستة، 7 footballs ٧ سبعة، 8 tennis balls ٨ ثمانية.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 42 to 43): colour circles for 7 toothbrushes and 6 paintbrushes; join spoons, plates, forks to ٦ ٧ ٨; ring the number for triangles, squares, circles; join umbrellas, cars, pots, carrots, cucumbers, tomatoes to ٦ ٧ ٨; **أفكر**: a park scene with children, balloons, footballs and birds on a branch: count each kind and shade bars.
- **أقرأ وأكتب** (pp. 44 to 45): 6 sorghum / millet ears (ذرة), 7 eggs in a basket, 8 corn cobs; trace ٦ (ice creams, **hoopoe stamps**, apples), ٧ (tennis balls, roosters, strawberries), ٨ (peppers, mangoes, tomatoes); **أفكر**: count books, rulers and bags on a shelf; count the circles of each colour.

## Lesson 2-4: الْعَدَدَانِ ٩، ١٠ (pp. 46 to 49)
- **أتعلم** (p. 46): 9 **pyramids**, 10 straw hats: ٩ تسعة، ١٠ عشرة.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 46 to 47): colour triangles for 9 pomegranates and squares for 10 strawberries; join sharpeners, erasers, pencils to ٨ ٩ ١٠; **count the letters of words and ring the number**: بيت سعاد / ذهبت للمسجد / أحب مدرستي (7, 9 or 10 letters; example 7 for بيت سعاد); join padlocks and keys to ٦ ٩ ١٠; **أفكر**: a garden with birds, bees and butterflies: count and shade bars.
- **أقرأ وأكتب** (pp. 48 to 49): 9 cars, 10 **Yemenia aircraft**; trace ٩ (sweets, paper clips, whistles) and ١٠ (sharpeners, hoopoe stamps, a domino with 10 dots); count limes; **أفكر**: a wardrobe with caps, yellow shirts, blue trousers, pink jackets: count each.

## Lesson 2-5: تَدْرِيبَاتٌ (pp. 50 to 51)
Colour as many circles / triangles / squares as the number (٧ ٦ ٨ ٩); draw circles for ٤ ٥ ٣; count beads of each colour on a necklace; count dots on dominoes; join the dots from ١ to ١٠ to draw a picture; count the pieces of each colour in a circle mosaic.

## Lesson 2-6: مُقَارَنَةُ الأَعْدَادِ (< ، > ، =) (pp. 52 to 53)
- **أتعلم** (p. 52): bead towers: ٣ أكبر من ٢; ٥ أصغر من ٧; ٤ تساوي ٤, written with the symbols. Note: in this RTL book the symbols are read right to left, so "٣ أكبر من ٢" is printed **٢ < ٣** (the open side faces the bigger number on the right).
- **أتدرب** (p. 52): ring the bigger number of two abacus rods (٥ / ٣, ٤ / ٦); ring the smaller of two towers (٧ / ٤, ٣ / ٢).
- (p. 53): ladybirds with spots: tick or cross the statement; turtles with spots: write < > = ; **أفكر**: fill in the number or the symbol (٩ … ٥، ٧ < …، ٦ > …، ٣ = …، ٣ < …، … = …، ٥ > …، ١٠ … ٩).

## Lesson 2-7: تَرْتِيبُ الأَعْدَادِ (ordering) (pp. 54 to 55)
- **أتعلم** (p. 54): an abacus with 0 to 10 beads on rods, labelled ٠ to ١٠.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 54 to 55): vases with 0 to 5 flowers, write the numbers (examples ٠ ١ ٣); bundles of pencils (4 to 7); fill in missing numbers in rows ٠ ١ … ٤ and ٦ … ٩; order three numbers like the example (٢ ٤ ٣ → ٢ ٣ ٤); **أفكر**: write three numbers in increasing order (٣ ٥ ١ → ١ ٣ ٥ example).

## Lesson 2-8: الأَعْدَادُ التَّرْتِيبِيَّةُ (ordinal numbers) (pp. 56 to 57)
- **أتعلم** (p. 56): five girls in school uniforms and white headscarves with bags board a bus in order: أروى، بلقيس، فاطمة، سارة، زينب.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 56 to 57): ten children walking in a line labelled الأول to العاشر (the teacher asks which colour is first, fourth…); colour the 4th, 6th and 8th rabbit; a bumper-car ride, ring the 3rd, 7th and 10th; ten animals (camel, dog, cat, chick, turtle, cow, hen, tiger, rabbit, goat): tick the 1st, 5th and 9th; **أفكر**: a race between a rabbit, a goat and a turtle: tick the fastest, ring the slowest.

## Lesson 2-9: حَلُّ الْمَسْأَلَةِ (p. 58)
A maze: join ٠ to ١٠ in order to find the way out.

## Lesson 2-10: تَدْرِيبَاتٌ عَامَّةٌ (pp. 59 to 61)
Complete colour patterns; complete a 0 to 10 number grid; fill in counting rows (٢ ٤ … ١٠ and ٩ ٧ … ١, counting by 2s forwards and backwards); colour shapes for ٥ ٣ ٧; count ears of grain, flowers, butterflies; tick or cross < > = statements; complete a triangle number puzzle; **ten children climbing stairs to a door, a house wall with two qamariya windows**: ring the right ordinal for each child.

## اختبار الوحدة (p. 62)
Ring the number of frogs and roosters; write the number of bananas and strawberries; fill in missing numbers ٠ to ١٠; tick or cross < > = ; colour the 3rd and 7th cat; complete a bead pattern.

## إعادة التعليم (p. 63)
Write the number of balls, circles, triangles; complete the number circles ٠ to ٨; ring the bigger number; ring the smaller number.

## إثراء (p. 64)
Join the dots in number order; write < > = in circles; read a **bar chart** (turtle 3, frog 5, rabbit 1) and write each animal's number.

## Unit 2 summary for the app
- Matches the app's current Math Unit 1 (counting) in spirit but goes much further: 0 and 10, writing digits, comparison with symbols, ordering, ordinal numbers, counting in 2s, a first bar chart.
- Digits must be **Eastern Arabic** (٠ to ٩), as in the book; check that the app's math activities use `lib/arabicNumbers.ts` everywhere.
- Comparison symbols are printed RTL (٢ < ٣ means "3 is bigger than 2"); the app must render and speak them the same way or children will be confused.
- New activity ideas: count and tap the number (`counting`, exists); write the digit (`trace` with digits); compare two numbers with three symbol buttons; put three numbers in order; "which one is 4th?" on a line of pictures; read a bar chart.
- Yemeni and local content: girls with Yemeni flags, Yemenia aircraft, hoopoe stamps, sorghum ears, Arab girls' names (أروى، بلقيس، فاطمة، سارة، زينب), a house wall with qamariya windows. The 9 pyramids (p. 46) are Egyptian, not Yemeni.
