# Unit 4: الطَّرْحُ ضِمْنَ الْعَدَدِ ١٠ (subtraction within 10)

Printed pp. 91 to 116 (PDF pp. 93 to 118). Eight lessons, then unit test, reteaching and enrichment. Subtraction is shown by **crossing out** objects (a diagonal stroke), and written RTL: "٥ − ٢ = ٣" with the words **٥ ناقص ٢ يساوي ٣**. Tables use the columns **العدد، ناقص، العدد، يساوي، الناتج**.

## Unit opener (p. 91)
A schoolyard in front of a yellow school building: eight children skip with ropes (girls in headscarves, boys), one boy and one girl walk away carrying their ropes. Talk: how many were skipping, how many left, how many are still skipping.

---

## Lesson 4-1: التَّهْيِئَةُ لِلطَّرْحِ (getting ready to subtract) (pp. 92 to 93)
- **أتعلم** (p. 92): four chicks on a branch, one flies away; an apple tree with apples, two have fallen; three flowers, a butterfly flies off. Teacher: talk about the whole, the part that left and **الباقي** (what remains).
- **أتدرب** (p. 93): three balloons, one bursts; five turtles, two go into the sea; four eggs, one cracked: write **الباقي**.

## Lesson 4-2: الطَّرْحُ ضِمْنَ ٥ (pp. 94 to 97)
- **أتعلم** (p. 94): five horses in a field, two leave through the open fence: **٥ ناقص ٢ يساوي ٣**, ٥ − ٢ = ٣.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 94 to 95): bees on flowers 4 − 2; five trees, one cut down; five candles, three blown out; four boys, one walks away; crossed-out circles 4 − 1, 5 − 3, 5 − 1, 4 − 4, 3 − 1, 2 − 2, 4 − 2, 3 − 0.
- (p. 96) **أتعلم**: a boy says **"لاحظ الجواب نفسه في الطريقتين"**: 5 − 2 = 3 written horizontally and vertically.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 96 to 97): 4 − 1 and 3 − 2 both ways; balloons 5 − 2; eight vertical sums; **أفكر**: missing numbers (٥ − … = ٣، ٣ − … = ١…).

## Lesson 4-3: حَقَائِقُ الطَّرْحِ ضِمْنَ ٥ (pp. 98 to 100)
- **أتعلم** (p. 98): two boys with marbles: "اطرح ١ من ٤: ٤ − ١ = ٣" and "اطرح ٢ من ٤: ٤ − ٢ = ٢".
- **أتدرب** (pp. 98 to 99): tables of all subtractions from 2, 3, 4 and 5 (2 − 1, 2 − 2; 3 − 1 … 3 − 3; 4 − 1 … 4 − 4; 5 − 1 … 5 − 5), using counters.
- (p. 100): a boy says **"أطرح أفقيًا ورأسيًا"**: dominoes 4 − 2 and 5 − 3; mixed facts both ways.
- (p. 101): marbles pouring out of jars (5 − 2, 3 − 1, 4 − 2, 5 − 0, 4 − 1, 5 − 3, 3 − 3); **أفكر**: join the subtractions that make ٢.

## Lesson 4-4: الطَّرْحُ ضِمْنَ ١٠ (pp. 102 to 103)
- **أتعلم** (p. 102): six boxes of pictures with crossed-out items joined to their sentence: 10 − 1 = 9, 10 − 3 = 7, 8 − 2 = 6, 7 − 0 = 7, 9 − 3 = 6, 6 − 6 = 0.
- **لنتسابق في الحل** (p. 103): a race track (a boy in a car with a Yemeni flag at the start, a monkey on roller skates, a trophy at **النهاية**): solve the vertical subtractions along the track.

## Lesson 4-5: حَقَائِقُ الطَّرْحِ ضِمْنَ ١٠ (pp. 104 to 107)
- **أتعلم** (p. 104): a boy with balloons "اطرح ٣ من ٧" and a girl in a yellow headscarf with flowers "اطرح ٢ من ٧".
- **أتدرب** (pp. 104 to 106): tables of every subtraction from 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 with counters.
- (p. 107): balloons 6 − 2, 7 − 4; vertical facts; mixed facts; join subtractions to ٤، ١، ٧; **أفكر**: balloon pictures, write the whole sentence.

## Lesson 4-6: حَلُّ الْمَسْأَلَةِ (pp. 108 to 109)
- A countryside scene (p. 108): hills, an apple tree with apples on the ground, a tree with five birds (one flying off), rabbits (two hopping away), boys playing football and two walking away: write subtraction sentences for birds (٥ − ١ = ٤ example), apples, rabbits and boys.
- (p. 109): stacks of bricks with some falling off: write the sentence (example ٥ − ٣ = ٢).

## Lesson 4-7: اكْتِشَافُ الأَنْمَاطِ (pp. 110 to 111)
- **أتعلم** (p. 110): 2 − 1 = 1, 3 − 1 = 2, 4 − 1 = 3 (each time one more).
- Columns 9 − 0 … 9 − 7; 3 − 3 … 10 − 3; 9 − 7, 8 − 6 … 2 − 0 (same difference): find the pattern.
- (p. 111): count back 10 9 8 … 1; complete shape-number patterns; **أفكر**: a subtraction triangle (٨ − ٣ = …).

## Lesson 4-8: تَدْرِيبَاتٌ عَامَّةٌ (pp. 112 to 113)
Triangles 4 − 1; pens 6 − 2; a girl in a headscarf with balloons 4 − 0; footballs 5 − 3; fifteen mixed facts; **أفكر**: butterflies "− ٥" and "− ٢" (subtract from each number on the wings); a star with ٣ in the centre (which subtraction gives 3?).

## اختبار الوحدة (p. 114)
Crossed-out pictures 3 − 1, 6 − 3; six facts; five vertical facts; write the sentence for tennis balls and triangles; complete the pattern ٢ ٤ ٦ … …

## إعادة التعليم (p. 115)
Eight crossed-out pictures to write the result (4 − 1, 9 − 4, 4 − 4, 8 − 5, 3 − 0, 7 − 6, 10 − 7, 5 − 0).

## إثراء (p. 116)
Join subtractions to ٢، ٣، ٦; fill in to make ٥; a caterpillar counting back by 2 (١٠ ٨ … ٠); a boy thinks **"عددي ينقص عن ٩ بـ ٣"**, write his number.

## Unit 4 summary for the app
- The app's `arithmetic` activity can show subtraction, but the book's method is **crossing out pictures** before symbols, and the word form "٥ ناقص ٢ يساوي ٣". A picture with tappable objects that the child crosses out would follow the book closely.
- New question types: what is left (الباقي); all facts from one number; missing number; same answer horizontally and vertically; count back by 1 and by 2; a riddle ("my number is 3 less than 9").
- Scenes: schoolyard with skipping ropes, horses leaving a field, a countryside picture to make several sentences.
