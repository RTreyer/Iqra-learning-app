# Unit 3: الْجَمْعُ ضِمْنَ الْعَدَدِ ١٠ (addition within 10)

Printed pp. 65 to 90 (PDF pp. 67 to 92). Nine lessons, then unit test, reteaching and enrichment. Sums are written RTL: **٣ + ٢ = ٥** is printed "٥ = ٢ + ٣" read from the right (first addend on the right). Vertical addition (الجمع الرأسي) has the first number on top and "+ second" below a line.

## Unit opener (p. 65)
A big tree; two doves on a branch and three flying in (2 + 3); three trees; two egg characters standing and two walking in (2 + 2). Talk about "how many altogether".

---

## Lesson 3-1: التَّهْيِئَةُ لِلْجَمْعِ (getting ready to add) (pp. 66 to 67)
- **أتعلم** (p. 66): three boys play football and two run to join; three chicks on a branch and one flying in; four turtles in the water and one on the beach. Teacher: talk about the pictures (who joins, how many now).
- **أتدرب** (p. 67): two rabbits and two more; a sheep and a ram; five ducks in a pond and one more: write the total.

## Lesson 3-2: الْجَمْعُ ضِمْنَ ٥ (pp. 68 to 71)
- **أتعلم** (p. 68): a **family at a round table**: father in a jacket and two women in headscarves sit, a girl and a boy walk in: **٣ زائد ٢ يساوي ٥**, ٣ + ٢ = ٥ (words and symbols).
- **أتدرب** (pp. 68 to 70): giraffes 1 + 3; flowers 4 in a vase + 1 in a hand; children 2 + 3; rabbits 3 + 2; **a girl in a green dress and white headscarf holding Yemeni flags** (4 + 1, 3 + 2); girls 1 + 1; apples 2 + 1; stars 3 + 1; cubes 2 + 2; birds on a branch 3 + 1; birds and tomatoes: write the whole sum; fact lists (١ + ٢، ٢ + ٢، ٣ + ٢، ١ + ٤، ٢ + ٣، ١ + ٤).
- (p. 71): join sums to ٣ ٤ ٥; **أفكر**: missing addend (٢ + … = ٣، … + ٤ = ٥…).

## Lesson 3-3: حَقَائِقُ الْجَمْعِ ضِمْنَ ٥ (number facts to 5) (pp. 72 to 73)
- **أتعلم** (p. 72): fact boxes: ٢ = ١ + ١; ٣ = ٢ + ١ = ١ + ٢ (apples on plates, the order does not matter); ٤ = ٣ + ١ = ٢ + ٢ = ١ + ٣ (four flowers).
- **أتدرب** (p. 72): ٤ = ٣ + … = … + ٣ (commutativity).
- (p. 73) **أتعلم**: three girls with flags: 4 + 1, 1 + 4, 3 + 2, 2 + 3 all make ٥. Fact box for 5; **أفكر**: ring the sums that make ٤ and ٥.

## Lesson 3-4: الْجَمْعُ ضِمْنَ ١٠ (pp. 74 to 75)
- **أتعلم** (p. 74): six boys in green football kit on a bench and two walking up: ٦ + ٢ = ٨; a plate of 5 apples and an empty plate: ٥ + ٠ = ٥ (adding zero).
- **أتدرب** (pp. 74 to 75): join sums to ٦ to ١٠; sums with 0; birds 4 + 3; twelve sums (٥ + ٤، ٨ + ٢، ٧ + ٣، ٩ + ٠…); **أفكر**: missing addends (٤ + … = ٦، ٦ + … = ٨، ٤ + … = ١٠…).

## Lesson 3-5: الْجَمْعُ الرَّأْسِيُّ ضِمْنَ ١٠ (vertical addition) (pp. 76 to 77)
- **أتعلم** (p. 76): 4 red and 3 yellow flowers: ٤ + ٣ = ٧ horizontally and vertically.
- **أتدرب** (pp. 76 to 77): balls 6 + 2, 2 + 6; marbles 3 + 5; vertical sums (٤ + ٦، ٠ + ١٠، ٧ + ٢، ٣ + ٢…); pairs that show order does not matter (٦ + ٢ / ٢ + ٦; ٣ + ٤ / ٤ + ٣; ٩ + ٠ / ٠ + ٩).

## Lesson 3-6: حَقَائِقُ الْجَمْعِ ضِمْنَ ١٠ (pp. 78 to 81)
- **أتدرب** (p. 78): 6 flowers coloured step by step: ٠ + ٦، ١ + ٥، ٢ + ٤، ٣ + ٣; 7 circles: ٠ + ٧ … ٧ + ٠ (all the ways to make 7).
- (p. 79): a flower with ٨ in the centre and petals "… + ١، … + ٢ …" (make 8); a star with ٩ in the centre (make 9).
- (p. 80): make ١٠: ٠ + …، ١ + … to ٥ + … and ١٠ + … to ٥ + …; ring the sums that make ٦، ٨، ١٠.
- (p. 81) **أفكر**: **part-part-whole** pentagons: ٣ and ٤ make ٧; fill in the missing part or whole; draw dots for the parts.

## Lesson 3-7: حَلُّ الْمَسْأَلَةِ (word problems from pictures) (pp. 82 to 83)
A boy pours balls from a bag into a basket; two baskets (5 balls and empty); yellow and blue shirts (4 + 4); a **girl in a white headscarf** and a **boy** each with school things (rulers, sharpeners, pens, a school bag): add the same things together.

## Lesson 3-8: اكْتِشَافُ الأَنْمَاطِ (patterns in sums) (pp. 84 to 85)
Cubes red and blue in rows of 5: ٥ + ٠، ٤ + ١ … ٠ + ٥; an addition table (+ ١ ٢ ٣ ٤ by ٤ ٥ ٦) with gaps; **أفكر**: number patterns ١ ٣ … … ٩ (odd numbers) and ٢ … ٦ … ١٠ (even); a "+١" table; a shape-number pattern.

## Lesson 3-9: تَدْرِيبَاتٌ عَامَّةٌ (pp. 86 to 87)
Domino sums (٥ + ٢…); circles in two colours (٦ + ٠، ٥ + ١ …); mixed sums; **أفكر**: a boy has ٧ pencils and a girl ٣: how many together? (write the sum sentence).

## اختبار الوحدة (p. 88)
Six horizontal and three vertical sums; chicks on a branch 5 + 2: write the sum sentence; sums showing order (٥ + ١ / ١ + ٥, ٤ + ٢ / ٢ + ٤); missing addends to ٦.

## إعادة التعليم (p. 89)
Apples ٣ + ١ = ٤ (example); stars ٥ + ٢; cubes ٢ + ٣; circles ٥ + ٥ vertical; triangles ٣ + ٥ vertical.

## إثراء (p. 90)
A diamond puzzle of sums with gaps: find the pattern (equal sums along rows).

## Unit 3 summary for the app
- Matches the app's current `arithmetic` activity, but the book stresses: pictures before symbols, the word form "٣ زائد ٢ يساوي ٥", adding zero, order does not matter, all ways to make 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, part-part-whole, vertical addition and missing addends.
- New question types worth adding: missing addend (… + ٣ = ٥); "which sums make ٨?" (tap all that fit); part-part-whole picture; a short picture word problem.
- Speak sums in the book's wording: "ثَلاثَةٌ زَائِدُ اثْنَيْنِ يُسَاوِي خَمْسَةً".
- Yemeni and family content: a family at the table, girls with Yemeni flags (again), boys in green football kit.
