# Unit 2: الْوَحْدَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ (first letters with fatha and long alif)

Printed pp. 36 to 59 (PDF pp. 37 to 60). Seven lessons, one letter each, then تدريبات.

Unit cover (p. 36): an open book with syllables on its pages: دَ، بَ، لَـ، رَ، مَا، تَ، بَا (right page) and رَا، نَ، تَا، دَا، لا، مَ، نَا (left page).

## Lesson pattern (same in every Unit 2 lesson)

Each lesson has three pages:

1. **Story page.** Letter badge in the top corner, lesson title, a large coloured picture, then short sentences written as a **rebus**: some words are replaced by a small black and white picture, and the child says the missing word (usually the verb). New words in blue, the lesson's key word in red.
2. **Writing page (أكتب).** A table of the letter in its forms, read right to left: initial (ـ start), medial, final connected, isolated. Header row: the model in black with a pink stroke-start mark. Row 1: grey letter to trace over. Row 2: outlined (hollow) letter to fill in. Row 3: dotted letter. Below a divider, the same table again as homework (house symbol).
3. **Reading page (أَنَا أَقْرَأُ).** Header shows the letter with fatha and with alif (e.g. "مَـ - مَا"). Boy and girl reading icons on each side.
   - ① a chain of hexagons with the letter in all positions with fatha, then with long alif
   - ② syllable building: "مَا/مَا" with an arrow to the word "مَامَا"
   - ③ two framed sight words to read
   - ④ ear symbol and a coloured picture for a **listening story** told by the teacher (text not printed)

App mapping: story page → `story`; writing page → `trace` per letter form (4 forms, not only isolated); ① and ② → `letter_pick` / new "syllable reading"; ③ → `word_read`; ④ → `story` (listen) with a recorded story plus `listen_pick`. The rebus is a new activity idea: "say or pick the missing word from the picture".

Notation below: `[picture: ...]` marks a rebus picture inside a sentence, with the word it stands for. **The rebus words are my reading of the picture, not printed in the book**; the teacher (or the teacher's guide) must confirm them before they are recorded.

## تَدْرِيبَات (Unit 2 exercises), pp. 58 to 59

1. **أَنَا أَقْرَأُ** (honeycomb of syllables): رَ، لَـ، تَا، مَا، دَ، نَا، تَ، مَ، بَا، لا، دَا، بَـ، رَا، نَ
2. **أَنَا أَقْرَأُ** (names and words built from the letters): مَنَالُ، مَرَامُ، نَمَلَاتُ، رَبَابُ، دَلَالُ، تَمَرَاتُ، مَتَابُ، مَامَا، بَابَا، مَالَا، دَارُ
3. **أَنَا أَقْرَأُ** (sight words of the unit): أَنَا، نَحْنُ، اللَّبَنُ، ثَلَاثُ، أُخْتِي، التَّمْرُ، أُحِبُّ، أَقْرَأُ، أَخِي، خَالِي، النَّمْلُ، الْمَوْزُ، تَمْرًا، قَالَ، أَعْطَتْ، الْحَمْدُ لِلّهِ
4. **أَنَا أَقْرَأُ الصَّوْتَ الأَوَّلَ** (read the first sound of each word): table of word and first sound

   | Word | First sound |
   |---|---|
   | بَدْرٌ | بَـ |
   | رَامِي | رَا |
   | تَمْرٌ | تَـ |
   | لَامَ | لَا |
   | دَارُ | دَا |
   | نَارُ | نَا |

5. **أَنَا أَكْتُبُ** (write the isolated form): مـ → م، بـ → ب، ـل → ل، ن، ـد → د، ر، ـم → م (connected forms given, child writes the letter under each).

## Unit 2 summary for the app

- **Letter order: م، ب، ل، ن، ر، د، ت** (with ة), each with fatha and long alif.
- Story texts are rebus sentences; the app can show the pictures inline and let the voice read the full sentence, or ask the child to pick the missing word.
- Every letter is practised in **all positions** (initial, medial, final, isolated), not only isolated.
- Syllable building (مَا / مَا → مَامَا) is a core activity the app does not have yet.
- Seven listening stories need full texts: fox and rooster, kite, scaffolding safety, fox with a bell, helping the old neighbour, hoopoe and ants, elephants.
- Sight words: أَنَا، أُحِبُّ، أَقْرَأُ، نَحْنُ، اللَّبَنُ، الْحَمْدُ لِلّهِ، ثَلَاثُ، النَّمْلُ، الْمَوْزُ، أُخْتِي، خَالِي، أَخِي، أَعْطَتْ، تَمْرًا، قَالَ، التَّمْرُ.
