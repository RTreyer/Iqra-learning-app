## 8-1 رَشِيدَةُ وَالْفَرَاشَاتُ (letter ش), pp. 169 to 174

### p. 169, story page
Letter badge: ش. Large picture: a girl in a white headscarf and light blue school dress sits on the grass under a big tree, smiling, a butterfly on her hand; colourful butterflies around her; a pink school bag beside her; red flowers; in the background a school with the Yemeni flag, a domed building and a brick house; brown hills.

Text (fully printed):
- فِي صَبَاحٍ مُشْرِقٍ ذَهَبَتْ رَشِيدَةُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ، تُرَدِّدُ أُنْشُودَةَ
- شُرُوقِ الْحُرِّيَّةِ. رَأَتْ رَشِيدَةُ فَرَاشَاتٍ تَنْتَشِرُ، فَجَرَتْ تُلَاحِقُ
- الْفَرَاشَاتِ.
- تَفَقَّدَتِ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ التَّلَامِيذَ، وَقَالَتْ: أَيْنَ رَشِيدَةُ؟
- قَالَتْ مَرْيَمُ: جَرَتْ تُلَاحِقُ الْفَرَاشَاتِ.
- قَالَتِ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ: يَا اللهُ ...! وَذَهَبَتْ تَبْحَثُ فِي الْحَدِيقَةِ، فَوَجَدَتْ
- رَشِيدَةَ جَالِسَةً تَحْتَ شَجَرَةٍ تُشَاهِدُ الْفَرَاشَاتِ.
- وَلَمَّا رَأَتْ رَشِيدَةُ الْمُعَلِّمَةَ وَقَفَتْ خَائِفَةً، وَقَالَتْ: أَنَا آسِفَةٌ.

Colours: مُشْرِقٍ، رَشِيدَةُ، أُنْشُودَةَ، شُرُوقِ، فَرَاشَاتٍ، تَنْتَشِرُ، شَجَرَةٍ، تُشَاهِدُ red; ذَهَبَتْ، الْحُرِّيَّةِ، تَبْحَثُ، خَائِفَةً، آسِفَةٌ blue.
Value: going straight to school, telling the teacher, apologising. Note: "أُنْشُودَة شُرُوق الْحُرِّيَّة" is a national song.

### p. 170, reading شَ - شَا
1. شَـ، ـشَـ، ـشَ، شَ، شَا، ـشَا
2. Cylinders: شَـ/جَـ/رَ/ةُ → شَجَرَةُ ; فَـ/رَا/شَا/تُ → فَرَاشَاتُ
3. Sight words: تَبْحَثُ، تُشَاهِدُ
4. Listening picture: a room on fire (the carpet and the curtain burning, smoke); a frightened boy fallen on the floor, a box of matches and burnt matches next to him. Safety story (never play with matches). Story text not printed.

### p. 171, writing ش
Forms: شـ، ـشـ، ـش، ش. Class and homework tables.

### p. 172, reading شُ - شُو
1. شُـ، ـشُـ، ـشُ، شُ، شُو، ـشُو
2. Cylinders: شُـ/رُو/قُ → شُرُوقُ ; أُنْ/شُو/دَ/ةُ → أُنْشُودَةُ
3. Sight words: خَائِفَةُ، الْحُرِّيَّةُ
4. Listening picture: an apple tree full of red apples; a boy in a blue shirt climbing it and breaking a branch; a boy in a yellow shirt below pointing up and calling out. Story about not damaging trees (or picking fruit without permission). Story text not printed.

### p. 173, word writing
Trace whole words: شمس، يمشي، مشمش، رموش. Class and homework tables.

### p. 174, reading شِ - شِي - شْ
1. شِـ، ـشِـ، ـشِ، شِ، شِيـ، ـشِيـ، شِي، ـشِي, then شْـ، ـشْـ، ـشْ، شْ
2. Fill the missing syllable: شِـ → تَنْـ/تَـ/.../رُ → تَنْتَشِرُ ; شِيـ → رَ/.../دَ/ةُ → رَشِيدَةُ ; مُشْـ → .../رِ/قُ → مُشْرِقُ
3. Sight words: ذَهَبَتْ، آسِفَةُ
4. Listening picture: school performance.

## 8-2 لَطِيفَةُ وَالنَّحْلَةُ (letter ط), pp. 175 to 180

### p. 175, story page
Letter badge: ط. Large picture: a girl in a blue headscarf, blue jacket and skirt runs away in fright, holding a stick; a bee flies after her; a big pink flower and red flowers near a tree; a house with an arched coloured window behind.

Text (fully printed):
- بَيْنَمَا لَطِيفَةُ تُشَاهِدُ طُيُورَ الْحَدِيقَةِ أَقْبَلَ النَّحْلُ يَطُوفُ
- فَوْقَ الزُّهُورِ.
- حَاوَلَتْ لَطِيفَةُ أَنْ تَطْرُدَ النَّحْلَ وَتَقْطِفَ الزُّهُورَ. طَارَتْ نَحْلَةٌ
- نَحْوَ لَطِيفَةَ، فَجَرَتْ تَصِيحُ: طَلَالُ ... طَلَالُ، النَّحْلَةُ تَطِيرُ
- وَرَائِي وَتُلَاحِقُنِي.
- ابْتَسَمَ طَلَالٌ، وَقَالَ: لَا تَقْتَرِبِي مِنَ النَّحْلِ، فَالنَّحْلُ قَدْ يَلْسَعُ
- مَنْ يَقْطِفُ الزُّهُورَ.

Colours: لَطِيفَةُ، طُيُورَ، يَطُوفُ، تَطْرُدَ، طَارَتْ، طَلَالُ، تَطِيرُ، يَقْطِفُ red; الزُّهُورِ، وَرَائِي، يَلْسَعُ blue.
Value: respecting nature (do not pick flowers or disturb bees).

### p. 176, reading طَ - طَا

3. Sight word: الزُّهُورُ
4. Listening picture: a kitchen; an orange cat runs off with a fish in its mouth; a boy in a striped shirt and a girl in a pink headscarf chase it; a red pot and plates fall and break; a bottle spills. Story text not printed.

### p. 177, writing ط
Forms: طـ، ـطـ، ـط، ط. Class and homework tables.

### p. 178, reading طُ - طُو
1. طُـ، ـطُـ، ـطُ، طُ، طُو، ـطُو
2. Cylinders: طُـ/يُو/رُ → طُيُورُ ; يَـ/طُو/فُ → يَطُوفُ
3. Sight words: وَرَائِي، النَّحْلَةُ
4. Listening picture: a tall tree with a nest of chicks on a high branch; a cat creeping along a lower branch toward a white dove; a girl in a pink jacket and blue headscarf below throws a pebble to scare the cat away. Story about protecting birds. Story text not printed.

## 8-3 زِيَارَةُ جَدِّي (letter ز), pp. 181 to 186

### p. 181, story page
Letter badge: ز. Large picture: a living room with a fridge and an arched coloured-glass window; the window glass is broken and the pink curtain blows outside; a blue football lies among the glass pieces; a boy in a green shirt raises his hands in shock; a grandfather in a white robe and cap with a walking stick stands by the door with a boy in a red-white striped jumper.

Text (fully printed):
- أَنَا وَأَخِي زِيَادٌ نَزُورُ جَدِّي يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ، فَيَقُصُّ لَنَا قِصَصًا جَمِيلَةً.
- وَفِي مَرَّةٍ سَمِعْنَا صَوْتَ زُجَاجٍ يَتَحَطَّمُ. قُمْنَا نَجْرِي، فَرَأَيْنَا
- - بِجِوَارِ أَخِي - كُرَةً بِلَوْنٍ أَزْرَقَ. قُلْتُ: يَا أَخِي لِمَ حَطَّمْتَ زُجَاجَ
- النَّافِذَةِ؟! قَالَ أَخِي: لَسْتُ أَنَا.
- طَرَقَ الْبَابَ زَائِرٌ. قُلْتُ مَنْ فِي الْبَابِ؟ قَالَ: أَنَا زَيْدٌ، فَتَحْتُ الْبَابَ،
- فَقَالَ زَيْدٌ أَنَا آسِفٌ، لَقَدْ حَطَّمَتْ كُرَةُ الْقَدَمِ زُجَاجَ النَّافِذَةِ.
- سَامَحَ جَدِّي زَيْدًا، وَتَأَسَّفْتُ لِزِيَادٍ، وَبَدَأْنَا نُزِيلُ الزُّجَاجَ.

Colours: زِيَادٌ، نَزُورُ، زُجَاجٍ، أَزْرَقَ، زَائِرٌ، زَيْدٌ، نُزِيلُ red; الْجُمُعَةِ، سَمِعْنَا، يَتَحَطَّمُ، النَّافِذَةِ، كُرَةُ blue.
Value: not blaming others without proof, admitting a mistake, forgiving, visiting grandparents.

### p. 182, reading زَ - زَا
1. زَ، ـزَ، زَا، ـزَا
2. Cylinders: زَيْـ/دُ → زَيْدُ ; زَا/ئِـ/رُ → زَائِرُ
3. Sight words: سَمِعْنَا، تَأَسَّفْتُ، الْجُمُعَةُ
4. Listening picture: three smiling flowers (purple, pink and red) with faces, standing in green grass. Story text not printed (a story told by flowers, possibly about sharing water or friendship).

### p. 183, writing ز
Non-connecting letter: ز، ـز in alternating columns. Class and homework tables.

### p. 184, reading زُ - زُو
1. زُ، ـزُ، زُو، ـزُو
2. Cylinders: زُ/جَا/جُ → زُجَاجُ ; نَـ/زُو/رُ → نَزُورُ
3. Sight words: النَّافِذَةُ، جَدِّي
4. Listening picture: a spotted hyena playing a flute (musical notes) on a sandy path; two brown goats dancing in front of it; red hills. Fable of the clever goat who asks the hyena (or wolf) to play music before eating him, and the music calls the shepherd's dogs. Story text not printed.

### p. 185, word writing
Trace whole words: زائر، نزور، أزرق، أرز. Class and homework tables.

### p. 186, reading زِ - زِي - زْ
1. زِ، ـزِ، زِيـ، ـزِيـ، زِي، ـزِي, then زْ، ـزْ
2. Fill the missing syllable: زِ → .../يَا/دُ → زِيَادُ ; زِيـ → نُـ/.../لُ → نُزِيلُ ; أَزْ → .../رَ/قُ → أَزْرَقُ
3. Sight words: كُرَةُ، يَتَحَطَّمُ
4. Listening picture: school performance.

## Unit 8 summary for the app

- **New letters: ش، ط، ز.** Total letters at the end of Part 1: **19** (م ب ل ن ر د ت ي و أ ق ص ف ح س ج ش ط ز). Not yet taught: ث خ ذ ض ظ ع غ ك هـ (9 letters), presumably in Part 2 (to confirm when Part 2 is read).
- Listening stories needing texts: the fire and the matches, the apple tree, the cat and the fish, the cat and the dove's nest, the talking flowers, the hyena's flute and the goats, plus the school performance.
- Values: punctuality, respecting nature, not blaming without proof, forgiveness, visiting grandparents on Friday.
- Places: شِبَام (Hadramawt).
