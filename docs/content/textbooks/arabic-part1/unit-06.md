# Unit 6: الْوَحْدَةُ السَّادِسَةُ (new letters ق، ص، ف)

Printed pp. 124 to 145 (PDF pp. 125 to 146). Three lessons, then تدريبات.

Unit cover (p. 124): an open book with: صِ، فُ، قَ، ـقْ، صَا، صْـ، قَا، قُو (right page) and فِـ، فُو، صِي، فْ، فَا، فِي، قِي، صُو (left page).

## What changes from Unit 5

- **New letters** (not met before): ق، ص، ف. Each lesson teaches one new letter with **all vowels in one lesson**: reading page per vowel group (fatha + long alif, damma + long و, kasra + long ي + sukun).
- Story texts are now **fully printed** (no rebus pictures).
- Reading page layout changes: syllables in round **pills** (green for fatha and damma pages, pink for kasra pages) instead of hexagons; ② becomes **cylinder pairs** (split word on top, whole word below) on fatha and damma pages, and a **fill-in-the-missing-syllable** exercise on kasra pages: the syllable is shown on top, the word with a gap "..." below it, and the complete word underneath.
- Sight words ③ are joined by a curved line (2 or 3 words).
- **Three listening pictures are the same** (pp. 130, 136, 142: a teacher presenting two groups of pupils at microphones, boys and girls). This looks like a recurring "presentation / song" activity: the pupils recite or sing (نشيد). Text not printed.

App mapping: fill-in-the-missing-syllable is a new activity type ("complete the word": pick the syllable that fills the gap).

## 6-1 مُنِيرٌ وَالْقِرَاءَةُ (letter ق), pp. 125 to 130

### p. 125, story page
Letter badge: ق. Large picture: a grocery shop (بَقَالَة); a shopkeeper in a white shirt with a jambiya (Yemeni dagger) at his belt spreads his hands behind the counter; shelves of bread, packets and pots; crates of onions, potatoes and tomatoes; a boy in a yellow jumper (مُنِير) holds out a note with writing; a boy in a red cap stands on the right.

Text (fully printed):
- أَعْطَتْ أُمِّي مُنِيرًا نُقُودًا وَوَرَقَةً، أَسْرَعَ أَخِي مُنِيرٌ إِلَى بَقَالَةِ لُقْمَانَ
- قُرْبَ الْبَيْتِ، أَخَذَ صَاحِبُ الْبَقَالَةِ النُّقُودَ، وَقَالَ: اقْرَأِ الْوَرَقَةَ.
- قَالَ أَخِي: يَا اللهُ! أَنَا لَا أَقْرَأُ، أَخَذَ وَلَدٌ الْوَرَقَةَ وَقَرَأَ
- (دَقِيقٌ، قِدْرٌ).
- شَكَرَ أَخِي الْوَلَدَ، وَقَالَ: يَجِبُ أَنْ أَتَعَلَّمَ الْقِرَاءَةَ.

Colours: نُقُودًا، وَرَقَةً، لُقْمَانَ، قُرْبَ، قَالَ، دَقِيقٌ، قِدْرٌ red; أَسْرَعَ، صَاحِبُ، يَجِبُ، أَتَعَلَّمَ، الْقِرَاءَةَ blue.
Value: why reading matters. Strong motivation story for the app's first ق lesson.

### p. 126, reading قَ - قَا
1. قَـ، ـقَـ، ـقَ، قَ، قَا، ـقَا
2. Cylinders: وَ/رَ/قَـ/ةُ → وَرَقَةُ ; قَا/لَ → قَالَ
3. Sight words: أَسْرَعَ، يَجِبُ
4. Listening picture: a man with a red-checked head cloth standing on a brick pouring a sack of grain into a grain mill; a full flour sack below; a girl in a pink headscarf and green dress reaching toward the running machine. Safety story (do not touch machines). Story text not printed.

### p. 127, writing ق
Forms: قـ، ـقـ، ـق، ق. Class and homework tables.

### p. 128, reading قُ - قُو
1. قُـ، ـقُـ، ـقُ، قُ، قُو، ـقُو
2. Cylinders: قُرْ/بَ → قُرْبَ ; نُـ/قُو/دُ → نُقُودُ
3. Sight words: أَتَعَلَّمُ، صَاحِبُ
4. Listening picture: a monkey in a cave lifting a metal dish lid that covered a hole full of leaves, looking shocked. Story text not printed.

### p. 129, word writing
Trace whole words: قرب، نقود، دقيق، برقوق. Class and homework tables.

### p. 130, reading قِ - قِي - قْ
1. قِـ، ـقِـ، ـقِ، قِ، قِيـ، ـقِيـ، قِي، ـقِي, then قْـ، ـقْـ، ـقْ، قْ
2. Fill the missing syllable: قِدْ → ... /رُ → قِدْرُ ; قِيـ → دَ/.../قُ → دَقِيقُ ; لُقْـ → .../مَا/نُ → لُقْمَانُ
3. Sight word: الْقِرَاءَةُ
4. Listening picture: a teacher in a green jumper presenting three girls in white headscarves and three boys in blue uniforms standing at microphones (school performance). Story text not printed.

## 6-2 صَابِرٌ وَالْبُلْبُلُ (letter ص), pp. 131 to 136

### p. 131, story page
Letter badge: ص. Large picture: a smiling boy in a light blue hoodie holding a wooden birdcage with a yellow bird inside; musical notes around (the bird sings).

Text (fully printed):
- ذَهَبَ أَخِي صَابِرٌ إِلَى سُوقِ صِيرَةَ يَشْتَرِي سَمَكَ التُّونَةِ، مَضَى
- الْوَقْتُ، وَلَمْ يَصِلْ صَابِرٌ، وَبَيْنَمَا نَحْنُ قَلِقُونَ وَصَلَ صَابِرٌ،
- وَأَعْطَى أَبِي بُلْبُلًا دَاخِلَ صُنْدُوقٍ وَقَالَ: أَنَا وَالْبُلْبُلُ أَصْدِقَاءُ
- يَا أَبِي، قَالَ أَبِي: أَيْنَ سَمَكُ التُّونَةِ يَا صَابِرُ؟ اعْتَذَرَ صَابِرٌ، وَقَالَ:
- أَتُرِيدُ أَنْ أَرُدَّ الْبُلْبُلَ؟
- قَالَ أَبِي مَنْصُورٌ: لَا، خُذِ النُّقُودَ وَأَحْضِرْ سَمَكَ التُّونَةِ.

Colours: صَابِرٌ، صِيرَةَ، يَصِلْ، وَصَلَ، صُنْدُوقٍ، أَصْدِقَاءُ، مَنْصُورٌ red; سُوقِ، يَشْتَرِي، سَمَكَ، التُّونَةِ، دَاخِلَ، اعْتَذَرَ، خُذِ، أَحْضِرْ blue.
Note: صِيرَة is a district (and fish market) in Aden. Value: doing the errand you were sent for, apologising.

2. Cylinders: صَا/بِـ/رُ → صَابِرُ ; وَ/صَـ/لَ → وَصَلَ
3. Sight words: دَاخِلَ، سَمَكٌ، التُّونَةُ
4. Listening picture: a park under a big tree; three boys playing football; two boys sitting back to back on a tree stump, arms crossed, sulking (they quarrelled). Story about making up after a quarrel. Story text not printed.

### p. 133, writing ص
Forms: صـ، ـصـ، ـص، ص. Class and homework tables.

### p. 134, reading صُ - صُو
1. صُـ، ـصُـ، ـصُ، صُ، صُو، ـصُو
2. Cylinders: مَنْـ/صُو/رُ → مَنْصُورُ ; صُنْـ/دُو/قُ → صُنْدُوقُ
3. Sight words: خُذْ، أَحْضِرْ، سُوقُ
4. Listening picture: a flock of white doves flying up together, lifting a hunter's net over them. The fable **الحمامة المطوقة** (the doves escape the net by flying together). Story text not printed.

## Unit 6 summary for the app

- **New letters: ق، ص، ف**, each with all vowels in one lesson. Total letters after Unit 6: 13.
- Stories are fully printed now: good material for the app's `story` and for comprehension questions.
- New activity: **fill in the missing syllable**.
- Places: سُوق صِيرَة (Aden). Culture: the jambiya, grocery shop, grain mill.
- Listening stories needing texts: the grain mill, the monkey and the lid, the two boys who quarrelled, the doves and the net, the wooden chest and the butterfly, the restaurant and the coins, plus the recurring school performance.
- Values: learning to read, finishing errands, apologising, solving problems (the mousetrap).
