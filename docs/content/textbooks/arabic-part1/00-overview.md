## Teaching approach (from المقدمة, p. 5)

- Develops the four skills: listening, speaking, reading, writing, plus thinking skills.
- **Phonics entry (المدخل الصوتي)**: letters are introduced by their sounds, following syllables (المقاطع الصوتية).
- Comprehension through **stand-alone stories and listening stories** (القصص المستقلة والقصص الاستماعية).
- **Letter order follows frequency** of letters in words children already use, simple to complex.
- Every lesson opens with a **picture that expresses the meaning**, so children grasp it before reading. Pictures inside unit stories are deliberately **not coloured**, so they do not distract from reading.
- Assessment: continuous (exercises, tests) and a final assessment.
- Companion guides: دليل المعلم، دليل الأسرة، دليل المدرسة.
- Values: Yemeni identity, national belonging, social values.
