# Unit 3: الْوَحْدَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ (ي، و، أ with all short vowels, long vowels and sukun)

Printed pp. 60 to 82 (PDF pp. 61 to 83). Three lessons, then تدريبات.

Unit cover (p. 60): an open book with: يُو، أَ، أو، وُو، وْ، يْـ، يَا، يَ، وَ (right page) and أُ، إِيـ، وُ، أْ، وِيـ، وِ، وَا (left page).

## What changes from Unit 2

- Each letter is now read with **fatha, damma, kasra and sukun**, and with the **long vowels** ا، و، ي (e.g. يَـ - يَا, يُـ - يُو, يْـ).
- Lessons are longer: one story page, one letter-writing page, **several reading pages** (one per vowel), one **word-writing page** (whole words to trace: e.g. يد، بيت، يبني، يدي).
- Story pages still use rebus pictures, but more of the text is now printed words.
- Rebus words below are my reading of the pictures (not printed); confirm with the teacher.

## 3-1 الْمُعَلِّمَةُ بَيَانُ (letter ي), pp. 61 to 66

### p. 61, story page
Letter badge: ي. Large picture: a teacher in a white headscarf writing on a whiteboard ("بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم" at the top), three boys in blue uniforms watching from their desks.

Text:
- الْمُعَلِّمَةُ بَيَانُ [picture: a hand holding a marker, word: تَكْتُبُ] الْكَلِمَاتِ (يُدَارُ - بَيْتٌ - يُولَدُ)،
- أَنَا أَكْتُبُ الْكَلِمَاتِ، أَخِي [picture: a boy sitting at his desk not writing, sad, word: لَا يَكْتُبُ] .
- الْمُعَلِّمَةُ [picture: the teacher's hand guiding a child's hand that holds a pencil, word: تُمْسِكُ] يَدَ أَخِي.
- أَخِي [picture: a child's hand writing in a notebook, word: يَكْتُبُ] (يُدَارُ - بَيْتٌ - يُولَدُ). شَكَرَ أَخِي الْمُعَلِّمَةَ بَيَانَ،
- نَحْنُ سُعَدَاءُ.

Colours: بَيَانُ red; الْكَلِمَاتِ، أَكْتُبُ، شَكَرَ، سُعَدَاءَ blue; the three words in brackets and يَدَ red.

### p. 62, writing ي (أَنَا أَكْتُبُ)
Forms: يـ، ـيـ، ـي، ي. Class and homework tables.

### p. 63, reading يَـ - يَا
1. يَـ، ـيَـ، يَ، يَا، ـيَا
2. بَ / يَا / نُ → بَيَانُ ; يَ / دُ → يَدُ
3. Sight words: الْمُعَلِّمَةُ، شَكَرَ
4. Listening picture: five boys holding colourful balloons and a kite in front of a mosque with a minaret and dome (Eid celebration). Story text not printed.

### p. 64, reading يُـ - يُو
1. يُـ، ـيُـ، يُ، يُو، ـيُو
2. يُـ / دَا / رُ → يُدَارُ ; يُو / لَـ / دُ → يُولَدُ
3. Sight words (with an arrow): أَكْتُبُ، الْكَلِمَاتُ
4. Listening picture: an old man in a striped turban and blue robe (Juha), a boy leading a white donkey by a rope, two men in turbans whispering and pointing at them, a palm tree. The classic story **جُحَا وَابْنُهُ وَالْحِمَار** (you cannot please everyone). Story text not printed.

### p. 65, word writing
Trace whole words: يد، بيت، يبني، يدي. Class and homework tables.

### p. 66, reading يْـ (sukun)
1. يْـ، يْ
2. بَيْـ / تُ → بَيْتُ
3. Sight word: سُعَدَاءُ
4. Listening picture: a rooster pecking a pile of grain alone while four white hens stand around it, flapping and complaining. Story about sharing (the selfish rooster). Story text not printed.

## 3-2 أَخِي مَرِيضٌ (letter و), pp. 67 to 73

### p. 67, story page
Letter badge: و. Large picture: a doctor with glasses and a stethoscope examining a sick boy lying in bed under a pink blanket.

Text:
- قَالَتْ وِدَادُ: بَابَا بَابَا أَخِي دَاوُودُ مَرِيضٌ، بَابَا [picture: father in a futa walking with a doctor who carries a bag, word: أَحْضَرَ] الطَّبِيبَ،
- الطَّبِيبُ [picture: the doctor sitting by the boy's bed examining him, word: فَحَصَ أَخِي] ، مَضَى يَوْمَانِ وَأَخِي [picture: the boy pushing away a spoon of medicine, word: لَا يَشْرَبُ الدَّوَاءَ] .
- خَالَتِي نَوَالُ أَعْطَتْ أَخِي وُرُودًا، وَنَاوَلَ خَالِي أَخِي [picture: a hand giving the boy a spoon, the boy accepting, word: الدَّوَاءَ] ،
- وَقَالَ: الطَّبِيبُ يُدَاوِينَا، وَاللهُ يَشْفِينَا.

Colours: وِدَادُ، دَاوُودُ، يَوْمَانِ، نَوَالُ، نَاوَلَ، يُدَاوِينَا red; مَرِيضٌ، الطَّبِيبَ، وَاللهُ يَشْفِينَا blue.
Value: taking medicine, visiting the sick.

### p. 68, reading وَ - وَا
1. وَ، ـوَ، وَا، ـوَا
2. نَا / وَ / لَ → نَاوَلَ ; نَ / وَا / لُ → نَوَالُ
3. Sight words: اللهُ، يَشْفِينَا
4. Listening picture: a sick man lying in a hospital bed (drip stand) seen through an arched window with coloured glass; a red dog lying patiently outside on the pavement below the window. Story about a loyal dog waiting for its owner. Story text not printed.

### p. 69, writing و
Non-connecting letter: و، ـو in alternating columns. Class and homework tables.

### p. 70, reading وُ - وُو
1. وُ، ـوُ، ـوُو
2. وُ / رُو / دُ → وُرُودُ ; دَا / وُو / دُ → دَاوُودُ
3. Sight words (with an arrow): الطَّبِيبُ، مَضَى
4. Listening picture: a boy on a flat rooftop crying and rubbing his eyes; an eagle (or hawk) flying away above him after dropping a chicken leg. Story text not printed.

### p. 71, reading وِ - وِي
1. وِ، ـوِ، وِيـ، ـوِيـ، ـوِي
2. وِ / دَا / دُ → وِدَادُ ; يُـ / دَا / وِيـ / نَا → يُدَاوِينَا
3. Sight words: مَرِيضٌ، قَالَتْ
4. Listening picture: a girl in a green headscarf with a red school bag walking on the pavement; a red car with its door open stopping next to her, a stranger inside. Safety story (do not get into a stranger's car). Story text not printed.

### p. 72, word writing
Trace whole words: وداد، يوم، ينمو، دود. Class and homework tables.

### p. 73, reading وْ (sukun)
1. ـوْ، وْ
2. يَوْ / مَا / نِ → يَوْمَانِ
3. Sight word: خَالَتِي
4. Listening picture: a boy talking happily on a red landline telephone, his mother in a pink headscarf standing behind him. Story about phone manners (or calling a relative). Story text not printed.

## 3-3 دَارُ الأَيْتَامِ (letter أ / إ, hamza on alif), pp. 74 to 80

### p. 74, story page
Letter badge: أ. Large picture: heavy rain and lightning over a two-storey brick orphanage; water flooding through the arched entrance; a woman in a purple robe and headscarf (the teacher إِيمَان) bailing water out with a basin, a girl in pink beside her doing the same.

Text:
- نَزَلَ [picture: the building in heavy rain, word: الْمَطَرُ] ، وَدَخَلَ الْمَاءُ دَارَ الأَيْتَامِ.
- أَسْرَعَتِ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ إِيمَانُ، وَقَالَتْ: يَا أُمَيْمَةُ نَادِي الْجِيرَانَ،
- وَأَخَذَتْ إِنَاءً، وَ [picture: the teacher scooping water with a basin at the door, word: أَخْرَجَتِ] الْمَاءَ.
- نَادَتْ أُمَيْمَةُ الْجِيرَانَ، أَصْلَحَ الْجِيرَانُ دَارَ الأَيْتَامِ.
- قَالَتْ أُمَيْمَةُ: نَحْنُ أَيْتَامٌ، وَالْمُعَلِّمَةُ إِيمَانُ أُمُّنَا.

Colours: إِيمَانُ، إِنَاءً، أُمَيْمَةُ، أَيْتَامٌ red; نَزَلَ، دَخَلَ، الْمَاءُ، أَسْرَعَتِ، أَخَذَتْ، الْجِيرَانَ، أَصْلَحَ blue.

### p. 76, writing أ
Forms: أ، إ، ـأ، ـأ (isolated with hamza above, with hamza below, and connected forms). Class and homework tables.

### p. 77, reading أُ - أُو

4. Listening picture: a fox with a key opening the padlock of a chicken coop, two hens inside frightened; a girl watching from a window of the house. Story text not printed.

### p. 78, reading إِ - إِيـ

## Unit 3 summary for the app

- **Letters: ي، و، أ (أ / إ)**, now with all four marks (fatha, damma, kasra, sukun) and the three long vowels.
- New skill: reading the **same letter with different vowels** (يَ يُ يِ يْ / يَا يُو يِي). The app's `letter_pick` only checks the letter shape; a "which vowel do you hear?" activity is needed.
- New skill: **tracing whole words**, not only letters.
- Eleven listening stories need texts: Eid balloons, Juha and the donkey, the selfish rooster, the loyal dog, the eagle and the boy, the stranger's car, the telephone, water tap, fox at the coop, bird in the rain, the lion and the animals.
- Values: helping a classmate, visiting and caring for the sick, caring for orphans, helping neighbours, safety with strangers.
