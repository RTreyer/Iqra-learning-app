# Unit 5: الْوَحْدَةُ الْخَامِسَةُ (ن، ر، د، ت again, with damma, kasra, sukun and long vowels)

Printed pp. 101 to 123 (PDF pp. 102 to 124). Four lessons, then تدريبات.

Unit cover (p. 101): an open book with: دِي، رْ، دْ، رُو، نَـ، نُو، دَا، ـتْ (right page) and تِي، تُو، تَـ، رِي، دِ، نِـ، رَا، نِي (left page).

Same pattern as Unit 4: story page, letter writing, reading pages per vowel with syllables-to-word matching (example arrow plus distractor), sight words, listening picture, word writing.
New in ③: some sight-word sets show **two smiley faces in the diamond grid** (p. 116, p. 121), meaning the child adds a word of their own or the teacher adds one.
Rebus words below are my reading of the pictures (not printed); confirm with the teacher.

## 5-1 تَرْتِيبُ الصَّفِّ (letter ن), pp. 102 to 106

### p. 102, story page
Letter badge: ن. Large picture: a classroom; pupils in light blue uniforms tidying: a boy carrying a chair, a boy at his desk, a girl in a white headscarf straightening a desk, a boy on a chair hanging a weather poster (sun, cloud, rain) with a girl helping him.

Text:
- [ear symbol and a bell picture, word: سَمِعَ] التَّلَامِيذُ رَنِينَ [bell, word: الْجَرَسِ] ، دَخَلَ التَّلَامِيذُ الصَّفَّ.
- قَالَتِ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ: نُرِيدُ تَرْتِيبَ الصَّفِّ.
- بَدَأَ مُنِيرٌ وَنِدَاءُ [picture: a boy and a girl arranging desks, word: بِتَرْتِيبِ الْمَقَاعِدِ] ، وَأَنْوَرُ وَنُورُ [picture: a boy and a girl hanging pictures on the wall, word: يُعَلِّقَانِ الصُّوَرَ] .
- وَلَمَّا رَأَتِ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ نَبِيلًا لَا يُسَاعِدُ التَّلَامِيذَ، أَسْرَعَتْ إِلَى تَرْتِيبِ الصَّفِّ.
- رَأَى نَبِيلٌ الْمُعَلِّمَةَ وَذَهَبَ يُسَاعِدُ التَّلَامِيذَ.
- شَكَرَتِ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ التَّلَامِيذَ وَرَوَتْ قِصَّةَ النَّمْلِ.

Colours: رَنِينَ، مُنِيرٌ، نِدَاءُ، أَنْوَرُ، نُورُ، نُرِيدُ، قِصَّةَ red; الصَّفَّ، تَرْتِيبَ، يُسَاعِدُ، رَأَى blue.
Value: cooperation, leading by example (the teacher starts, the idle pupil follows). Links back to the ants story in 2-4.

### p. 103, writing ن
Forms: نـ، ـنـ، ـن، ن. Class and homework tables.

### p. 104, reading نُ - نُو
1. نُـ، ـنُـ، ـنُ، نُ، نُو، ـنُو
2. Split words: نُـ/رِيـ/دُ، نُو/رُ ; whole words: نُورُ، النَّمْلُ، نُرِيدُ (example arrow نُـ/رِيـ/دُ → نُرِيدُ; النَّمْلُ is a distractor)
3. Sight words (with an arrow): الصَّفُّ، تَرْتِيبُ، رَأَى
4. Listening picture: two white geese on a sandy shore, one looking proud, the other with its head turned away; swans and ducks swimming in the lake behind; a palm tree. Story text not printed.

### p. 105, word writing
Trace whole words: نحن، منير، أمين، يومان. Class and homework tables.

### p. 106, reading نِـ - نِي - نْـ
1. نِـ، ـنِـ، ـنِ، نِ، نِيـ، ـنِيـ، ـنِي، نِي, then نْـ، ـنْـ، ـنْ، نْ
2. Split words: رَ/نِيـ/نُ، نِـ/دَا/ءُ، أَنْـ/وَ/رُ ; whole words: أَنْوَرُ، رَوَتْ، رَنِينُ، نِدَاءُ (example arrow رَ/نِيـ/نُ → رَنِينُ; رَوَتْ is a distractor)
3. Sight words (diamond shapes): قِصَّةٌ، يُسَاعِدُ
4. Listening picture: two rabbits in a meadow; one lies on its back laughing and kicking, the other stands pointing and laughing. Story text not printed (possibly the hare who mocks, before "the tortoise and the hare").

## 5-2 رَجُلُ الْمُرُورِ (letter ر), pp. 107 to 111

### p. 107, story page
Letter badge: ر. Large picture: a traffic policeman in a blue uniform, cap and yellow vest blowing a whistle and raising his hand to stop a pink car; a girl in a pink headscarf and green dress about to cross; a lorry and a car behind.

Text:
- بَيْنَمَا أُخْتِي رُبَا [picture: a girl buying flowers and water from a shopkeeper at a kiosk, word: تَشْتَرِي] وَرْدًا وَمَاءً بَارِدًا،
- أَسْرَعَتْ أُخْتِي رِيمُ [picture: a girl stepping onto the road, word: تَعْبُرُ الشَّارِعَ] وَفَجْأَةً أَتَتْ [picture: a car speeding toward the girl, word: سَيَّارَةٌ] .
- شَاهَدَ رَجُلُ الْمُرُورِ رِيمَ، وَصَاحَ: قِفْ! قِفْ!. وَأَخَذَ يَدَ
- رِيمَ وَ [picture: the policeman walking the girl across a zebra crossing, word: عَبَرَ بِهَا الشَّارِعَ] . شَكَرَتْ رِيمُ رَجُلَ الْمُرُورِ، وَأَعْطَتْ رُبَا رَجُلَ الْمُرُورِ وَرْدَةً.

Colours: رُبَا، بَارِدًا، رَجُلُ الْمُرُورِ، رِيمَ، وَرْدَةً red; فَجْأَةً، شَاهَدَ، صَاحَ، قِفْ blue.
Value: road safety, crossing with care, thanking helpers.

### p. 108, writing ر
Non-connecting letter: ر، ـر in alternating columns. Class and homework tables.

### p. 109, reading رُ - رُو
1. رُ، ـرُ، رُو، ـرُو
2. Split words: رُ/بَا، مُـ/رُو/رُ ; whole words: مُرُورُ، رُبَا، مَرْوَانُ (example arrow مُـ/رُو/رُ → مُرُورُ; مَرْوَانُ is a distractor)
3. Sight words (with an arrow): رَجُلٌ، شَاهَدَ، فَجْأَةً
4. Listening picture: an angry ram facing a large mirror, snorting at its own reflection (about to charge). Story text not printed.

### p. 110, word writing
Trace whole words: ريم، تريم (row 1 shows منير instead of تريم; probably a print error), مرور، دار. Class and homework tables.
Note: تَرِيم is a historic city in Hadramawt.

### p. 111, reading رِ - رِي - رْ
1. رِ، ـرِ، رِيـ، ـرِيـ، ـرِي، رِي, then رْ، ـرْ
2. Split words: بَا/رِ/دُ، رِيـ/مُ، وَرْ/دَ/ةُ ; whole words: رِيمُ، وَرْدَةُ، بَارِدُ، مُرُورُ (example arrow بَا/رِ/دُ → بَارِدُ; مُرُورُ is a distractor)
3. Sight words (diamond shapes): صَاحَ، قِفْ
4. Listening picture: countryside; a farmer with a hoe working a field, a woman picking fruit from a tree, a mother and child walking on a path toward houses on a hill; three yellow birds flying. Story text not printed.

## 5-3 بَدْرٌ وَاللُّعْبَةُ (letter د), pp. 112 to 116

### p. 112, story page
Letter badge: د. Large picture: a purple Ferris wheel with children in the cabins; a teacher with glasses and a moustache in the foreground; a boy (بَدْر) standing alone at the fence, looking up.

Text:
- ذَهَبَ الْمُعَلِّمُ وَالتَّلَامِيذُ إِلَى مَدِينَةِ [picture: an amusement park with a Ferris wheel and rides, word: الأَلْعَابِ] ،
- اخْتَارَ نَادِرٌ [picture: children riding a dog-shaped train, word: الْقِطَارَ] ، وَاخْتَارَ بَدْرٌ وَدُنْيَا الدَّوَّارَ.
- وَبَيْنَمَا اللُّعْبَةُ تَدُورُ، رَأَى الْمُعَلِّمُ بَدْرًا خَارِجَ اللُّعْبَةِ.

Colours: مَدِينَةِ، نَادِرٌ، بَدْرٌ، دُنْيَا، تَدُورُ red; اخْتَارَ، اللُّعْبَةُ، خَارِجَ، أَعْطَى blue.

## 5-4 مَنَالُ وَالْمَزْرَعَةُ (letter ت), pp. 117 to 121

### p. 117, story page
Letter badge: ت. Large picture: a girl in a pink dress and headscarf (مَنَال) watering fruit trees in a farm with a hose; hills behind, birds flying.

Text:
- ذَهَبَ خَالِي إِلَى [picture: the Kaaba in Mecca surrounded by pilgrims, word: الْحَجِّ / مَكَّةَ] دُونَ أَنْ يَرْوِيَ الْمَزْرَعَةَ، مَرَّتْ
- مَنَالُ بِنْتُ خَالِي بَيْنَ تِلَالِ الْوَادِي وَرَأَتْ نَبَاتَ الْمَزْرَعَةِ مِنْ تِينٍ وَتُوتٍ
- يَحْتَاجُ إِلَى مَاءٍ. أَسْرَعَتْ مَنَالُ وَ [picture: Manal watering the trees with a hose, word: رَوَتْ] نَبَاتَ الْمَزْرَعَةِ.
- عَادَ خَالِي وَرَأَى الْمَزْرَعَةَ خَضْرَاءَ. قَالَتْ خَالَتِي: مَنَالُ [picture: Manal watering, word: رَوَتْ] نَبَاتَ الْمَزْرَعَةِ، فَرِحَ خَالِي، وَأَعْطَى مَنَالَ

Colours: تِلَالِ، تِينٍ، تُوتٍ، تُمُورِ، أَتْلُوَ red; الْمَزْرَعَةَ، يَحْتَاجُ، عَادَ، خَضْرَاءَ، فَرِحَ، الْقُرْآنَ blue.

## Unit 5 summary for the app

- **Letters revisited: ن، ر، د، ت (ة)** with all vowels and sukun. After Unit 5 the child has met all 10 letters of Part 1 (م ب ل ن ر د ت ي و أ) with all short and long vowels.
- Stories are longer (5 to 6 lines) with fewer rebus pictures.
- Places and culture: Tarim, Hajj / Mecca, the Prophet's Mosque in Medina, Quran recitation, the amusement park.
- Listening stories needing texts: the two geese, the laughing rabbits, the ram and the mirror, the countryside, the sleeping lion and the elephant, the king and the monkey, the drowned hen, the sick cat at the clinic.
- Values: tidying together, road safety, sharing help, responsibility on the farm, love of the Quran.
