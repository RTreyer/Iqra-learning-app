# Unit 1: الْوَحْدَةُ الأُولَى (pre-reading)

Printed pp. 9 to 35 (PDF pp. 10 to 36). Five lessons 1-1 to 1-5 without titles. The unit has **no printed text for the child**: every page is pictures with an activity symbol. The teacher (and in the app: the voice) gives the words. Goals: naming and describing pictures, listening and recognising sounds, odd one out, matching, good and bad behaviour, pencil grip and sitting posture, pre-writing lines.

Unit cover (p. 9): a yellow-green songbird (finch or canary, grey head, black and white wings) on a red-brown twig. Same bird opens lesson 1-1.

Symbols are those of the book (see `00-overview.md`). "Position" is given as the child sees the page (right column is read first).

### p. 11, أنا أستمع إلى الأصوات وأسميها (listen to sounds and name them), symbol: boy with hand at ear
The same eight animals rearranged: dog, cat, calf, goat (right column); songbird, crow, hen, rooster (left column). The teacher plays or imitates each animal's sound; the child points to the animal and names it. Sounds: نُبَاح (dog), مُوَاء (cat), خُوَار (calf/cow), ثُغَاء (goat), تَغْرِيد (bird), نَعِيق (crow), قَوْقَأَة (hen), صِيَاح (rooster).
App: sound recordings needed (animal sounds), then `listen_pick` with pictures.

### p. 12, matching (arrow): mother and young
Two columns. Right column: hen, cat (drawing, black and white), cow grazing on grass, lion with mane standing on a rock. Left column: calf, lion cub, chick, kitten. A worked example arrow goes from the hen to the chick. The child draws arrows: hen → chick (كَتْكُوت), cat → kitten (هُرَيْرَة), cow → calf (عِجْل), lion → lion cub (شِبْل).
App: `matching` (adult animal to its young).

## Lesson 1-4: family scenes, putting things back, behaviour, step lines (pp. 26 to 30)

1. Top: a Yemeni family eating together on the floor (mafraj style): father in white sitting cross-legged in the middle, mother in a pink patterned headscarf passing a hot clay pot (soup or saltah), a small girl in a white headscarf, two boys eating with spoons; dishes on a green cloth (bread, chicken, salad, rice, cups), a teapot on a tray, a basket of flat bread.
2. Bottom: a family sitting together in a living room: grandfather with a white cap and glasses holding a small girl, a boy on his father's lap, father in a shirt and futa, mother knitting with purple wool, grandmother in a pink headscarf holding a baby.
Teaching role: family words (أَب، أُمّ، جَدّ، جَدَّة، أَخ، أُخْت), describing a scene in sentences, values (eating together, respect for grandparents).

### p. 27, listen: family talk (two large pictures)

## Lesson 1-5: hand directions, introducing myself, listening story, reading pose, circles (pp. 31 to 35)

### p. 31, hand signs for directions (no symbol, teacher game)
Hands with a pointing index finger in four directions: two hands and one hand pointing up; two hands and one hand pointing left; two hands and one hand pointing down; at the bottom one closed fist.
Teaching role: direction words for writing (فَوْق، تَحْت، يَمِين، يَسَار) and a stop sign (fist). Teacher says a direction, children point.
App: `listen_pick` "where does the finger point?" with arrow pictures.

### p. 32, about me: portraits with a name line
A boy in a red-brown shirt (framed portrait) with an empty grey name box beside it; a girl in a grey headscarf and blue pinafore (framed portrait) with an empty name box. Teaching role: the child says his or her name ("اسْمِي ..."), the teacher writes it in the box; recognising one's own name in writing.
App: optional "اسمي" screen (child records or picks an avatar), no scoring.

### p. 33, listening story (ear symbol) and an open book
Top: a brown dog with a bone in its mouth standing at the edge of a pond, looking at its reflection (which also holds a bone); trees, grass, two orange rocks. This is the classic fable **الكلب والعظمة** (the greedy dog who drops his bone to grab the one in the reflection). The teacher tells the story; the text is not printed.
Bottom: an empty open book and an empty name box (child names the book or the story).
App: `story` (listen) with the fable told by the recorded voice, then `listen_pick` comprehension questions. **The story text must be written** (not in the book), for example from the teacher's guide.

### p. 34, reading poses with name boxes
Four children with books, each with an empty grey box: boy reading a yellow book; boy at a desk raising a book (same as the cover); girl in a black headscarf reading an orange book; girl in a black headscarf holding a book and smiling. A horizontal rule splits boys (top) and girls (bottom).
Teaching role: "أنا أقرأ" pose, saying a short sentence about each child (الْوَلَدُ يَقْرَأُ / الْبِنْتُ تَقْرَأُ).

### p. 35, lines: circles
Model circle starting at a dot on the right, drawn **counter-clockwise** (up, over the top, down the left, along the bottom), red arrows. Three dotted circles in class; homework: two dotted circles, then a framed box with one faint circle and one empty frame to draw a circle freely.
App: `trace` with a circle guide, start dot on the right, counter-clockwise.

## Unit 1 summary for the app

- Activity types used by the book: name pictures, listen to sounds (non-speech audio), match (arrow), behaviour ✓/✗, odd one out, put things back, line tracing (curves, straight, corners, steps, circles), listening story, talking about scenes.
- **Missing in the app today**: real animal, vehicle and human sound recordings; odd one out; put things back; the dog and bone listening story; posture and pencil grip; direction game.
- Vocabulary to cover: animals and their young, farm birds, vehicles, national symbols (flag, emblem), feelings, family members, school and mosque manners.
- Pictures are photos or coloured drawings. For the app use emoji where they are clear (🐐 🐄 🐕 🐈 🐦 🐔 🐓 🦁 🐥 ✈️ 🚗 🚑 🏍️ 🥁 🍎 🍇 🕌 🏫 ⚽ 🏀 ✏️ 🖊️) and new illustrations for scenes (family meal, behaviour pictures, shelves).
