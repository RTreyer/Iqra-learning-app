# Unit 4: الْوَحْدَةُ الرَّابِعَةُ (م، ب، ل again, with damma, kasra, sukun and long vowels)

Printed pp. 83 to 100 (PDF pp. 84 to 101). Three lessons, then تدريبات.

Unit cover (p. 83): an open book with: بْـ، مَـ، لِـ، لْـ، بُو، مُو، مِـ، مْـ (right page) and مِي، بُـ، لُو، مُـ، لِي، بِـ، لُـ، بِي (left page).

Pattern as in Unit 3: story page, letter writing, reading pages per vowel (① syllable chain, ② **matching syllables to words**: a framed list of split words on the right, a list of whole words on the left, one example arrow printed, the child draws the other arrows), ③ sight words, ④ listening picture, plus a word-writing page.

New exercise in ②: the split word and the whole word are in **different orders**, so the child must read to match (in Units 2 and 3 there was a single arrow).
App: `matching` (syllables to word) fits directly.

Rebus words below are my reading of the pictures (not printed); confirm with the teacher.

## 4-1 بَيْتٌ مِنْ رَمْلٍ (letter م), pp. 84 to 88

### p. 84, story page
Letter badge: م. Large picture: on a beach, a teacher in a green shirt and sunglasses with four children around a large sandcastle: a boy with swimming goggles on his head, a curly-haired boy, a boy in a pink shirt kneeling, a girl in a yellow sun hat and pink headscarf. Sea with a sailing boat, a red crab in the sand.

Text:
- ذَهَبَ الْمُعَلِّمُ مُرَادُ وَالتَّلَامِيذُ إِلَى [picture: teacher and children swimming and playing in the sea, a bus on the shore, word: شَاطِئِ / بَحْرِ] الْمَهْرَةِ.
- مَأْمُونٌ وَتَامِرٌ [picture: two boys throwing a beach ball on the shore, word: يَلْعَبَانِ بِالْكُرَةِ] . أَمِينٌ وَأَمَلُ [picture: a boy and a girl in a sun hat building a sand house, word: يَبْنِيَانِ بَيْتًا]
- مِنْ رَمْلٍ، [picture: the ball landing on the sand house and breaking it, word: الْكُرَةُ هَدَمَتِ] الْبَيْتَ، [picture: the boy and the girl looking sad at the ball and the broken sand house, word: حَزِنَ] أَمِينٌ وَأَمَلُ،
- أَصْلَحَ تَامِرٌ وَمَأْمُونٌ الْبَيْتَ. نَحْنُ سُعَدَاءُ.

Colours: مُرَادُ، مَأْمُونٌ، تَامِرٌ، أَمِينٌ، أَمَلُ، رَمْلٍ red; ذَهَبَ الْمُعَلِّمُ، التَّلَامِيذُ، الْمَهْرَةِ، الْبَيْتَ blue.
Note: الْمَهْرَة is a governorate on Yemen's eastern coast. Value: fixing what you broke.

### p. 85, writing م
Forms: مـ، ـمـ، ـم، م (traced with the pink start mark). Class and homework tables.

### p. 86, reading مُ - مُو
1. مُـ، ـمُـ، ـمُ، مُ، مُو، ـمُو
2. Split words: مُـ/رَا/دُ، مَأْ/مُو/نُ ; whole words: مَأْمُونُ، مُرَادُ، مَامَا (example arrow مُـ/رَا/دُ → مُرَادُ; مَامَا is a distractor)
3. Sight words (with an arrow): ذَهَبَ، الْبَيْتُ، الْمَهْرَةُ
4. Listening picture: a farmer in a blue robe cutting green crops with a sickle in a field; a woman milking a cow next to an old tower house; brown hills. Story about farm work. Story text not printed.

### p. 87, word writing
Trace whole words: مراد، إيمان، ريم، نوم. Class and homework tables.

### p. 88, reading مِـ - مِي - مْـ
1. مِـ، ـمِـ، ـمِ، مِ، مِيـ، ـمِيـ, then مْـ، ـمْـ، مْ
2. Split words: تَا/مِـ/رُ، أَ/مِيـ/نُ، رَمْـ/لُ ; whole words: رَمْلُ، أَمِينُ، تَامِرُ، مَأْمُونُ (example arrow تَا/مِـ/رُ → تَامِرُ)
3. Sight words (diamond shapes): الْمُعَلِّمُ، التَّلَامِيذُ
4. Listening picture: a classroom; a teacher in a black abaya and headscarf at the blackboard; a boy with a blue backpack standing at the open door (arriving late); pupils in green uniforms at their desks looking at him. Story about being on time. Story text not printed.

## 4-2 ابْنَةٌ أَمِينَةٌ (letter ب), pp. 89 to 93

### p. 89, story page
Letter badge: ب. Large picture: an old-city street with tall houses; a man in a futa and jacket with his son talking to a girl in a white headscarf and pink dress (أَبْرَار) who is holding out her hand; two boys sitting on the steps of a house behind.

Text:
- وَجَدَتْ أَبْرَارُ [picture: a hand picking up a Yemeni banknote, word: نُقُودًا] أَمَامَ بُيُوتِ الْجِيرَانِ.
- نَادَتْ: مَنْ أَضَاعَ النُّقُودَ؟
- قَالَ بِلَالُ: نَبِيلٌ أَضَاعَ النُّقُودَ.
- قَالَتْ أَبْرَارُ: كَمِ النُّقُودُ؟
- قَالَ نَبِيلٌ: [picture: a Yemeni 1000 rial banknote, word: أَلْفُ رِيَالٍ] .
- أَخَذَ نَبِيلٌ النُّقُودَ وَشَكَرَ أَبْرَارَ.
- قَالَ أَبُو نَبِيلٍ: أَبْرَارُ أَمِينَةٌ.

Colours: أَبْرَارُ، بُيُوتِ، بِلَالُ، نَبِيلٌ، أَبُو red; وَجَدَتْ، أَضَاعَ، كَمِ النُّقُودُ، شَكَرَ blue.
Value: honesty (الأَمَانَة). The banknote can be shown as a generic note in the app.

### p. 90, writing ب
Forms: بـ، ـبـ، ـب، ب. Class and homework tables.

### p. 91, reading بُ - بُو
1. بُـ، ـبُـ، ـبُ، بُ، بُو، ـبُو
2. Split words: بُـ/يُو/تُ، أَ/بُو ; whole words: أَبُو، بُيُوتُ، نَبَاتُ (example arrow بُـ/يُو/تُ → بُيُوتُ; نَبَاتُ is a distractor)
3. Sight words (with an arrow): أَخَذَ، وَجَدَتْ، أَضَاعَ
4. Listening picture: a mother in a white headscarf sitting on a sofa wiping a dirty smudge from her son's face; he looks upset. Story about cleanliness (or a fall). Story text not printed.

### p. 92, word writing
Trace whole words: بدر، بلبل، لبيب، باب. Class and homework tables.

### p. 93, reading بِـ - بِي - بْـ
1. بِـ، ـبِـ، ـبِ، بِ، بِيـ، ـبِي, then بْـ، ـبْـ، ـبْ، بْ
2. Split words: نَ/بِيـ/لُ، بِـ/لَا/لُ، أَبْـ/رَا/رُ ; whole words: بِلَالُ، بِلَادُ، نَبِيلُ، أَبْرَارُ (example arrow نَ/بِيـ/لُ → نَبِيلُ; بِلَادُ is a distractor)
3. Sight words (diamond shapes): كَمْ، النُّقُودُ
4. Listening picture: a girl in a pink patterned headscarf opening the door of a birdcage; a red bird flying out. Story about freeing a bird. Story text not printed.

## 4-3 الْبَقَرَةُ (letter ل), pp. 94 to 98

### p. 94, story page
Letter badge: ل. Large picture: a brown cow lying on green grass with her newborn calf beside her.

Text (almost fully printed):
- رَأَتْ أُخْتِي لِينَا الْبَقَرَةَ مَرِيضَةً. نَادَتْ: أُمِّي.. أُمِّي، الْبَقَرَةُ مَرِيضَةٌ.
- أَسْرَعَتْ أُمِّي وَرَأَتِ الْبَقَرَةَ تَلِدُ.
- سَاعَدَتْ أُمِّي الْبَقَرَةَ وَ [picture: a hand holding a bucket for the cow to drink, word: أَحْضَرَتْ] دَلْوَ مَاءٍ.
- وَلَدَتِ الْبَقَرَةُ مَوْلُودًا. شَكَرَتْ أُمِّي أُخْتِي لِينَا.
- نَحْنُ سُعَدَاءُ بِمَوْلُودِ الْبَقَرَةِ.

Colours: لِينَا، أُمِّي (first)، تَلِدُ، دَلْوَ، مَوْلُودًا red; الْبَقَرَةَ، مَرِيضَةٌ، أُمِّي (second)، سَاعَدَتْ، شَكَرَتْ blue.
Value: caring for animals, telling an adult.

## Unit 4 summary for the app

- **Letters revisited: م، ب، ل** with damma, kasra, sukun, long و and long ي.
- Spiral design: letters return in later units with new vowels. The app's "one unit = three letters" model does not show this; a letter can appear in several units.
- Reading ② is now **syllables-to-word matching with distractors**: a direct fit for the app's `matching`.
- Listening stories needing texts: farm work, late for class, cleaning the face, freeing the bird, the lion and the animals, the cat pretending to be ill.
- Values: repairing what you broke, honesty (returning lost money), caring for animals.
- Place names: الْمَهْرَة (Yemeni governorate).
